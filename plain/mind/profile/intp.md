# INTP — The Cognitive Function Stack

**The short version.** Dan types as an INTP on every personality instrument he has ever taken, and the numbers are extreme enough that the four-letter label matters less than what the scores actually say. His dominant function — introverted thinking — sits at the 96th percentile. His auxiliary intuition is at 84th. His feeling function, the one that governs social connection and emotional trust, sits at the 10th percentile. This is not the cheerful "scientist" INTP of internet type descriptions. Every function runs darker.

## The five functions, measured

| Function | Score | What it means |
|----------|-------|---------------|
| Ti (dominant) | 96% latent / 95% aptitude | Lead logic. Builds frameworks from first principles. |
| Ne (auxiliary) | 84% latent / 80% valuing | Exploratory. Scans for patterns and edge cases. |
| Ni (leak) | 84% latent | Unusually high for an INTP. Reads coincidence as fate. |
| Si (tertiary) | 57% latent | High-fidelity memory. Also carries the trauma material. |
| Fe (inferior) | 10% valuing / 46% active | Feeling function — weak, distrusted, but desperately craved. |

## Ti — the existential scalpel

Dominant introverted thinking is not applied to mechanical puzzles. Dan points it at the self, social systems, and the cosmos. He engineers frameworks from first principles and defines truth as "a system that holds under recursive collapse, not social consensus" — he trusts only what he can simulate in his own head, running "internal simulation labs" where ideas, relationships, and futures are stress-tested.

The scalpel strips social niceties, dogma, and comforting illusion on principle. It follows logical chains to their conclusions even when those conclusions are psychologically devastating. Injustice registers as logical inconsistency before it registers as feeling. The documented outputs: philosophical nihilism as a default resting state, compulsive system deconstruction (exposing legal, bureaucratic, and social conventions as arbitrary machines), and the evidence-first analytical practice that runs across the whole archive.

## Ne — the sniper scout

Auxiliary extraverted intuition is described as *selective divergence*, not brainstorm spray. It explores conceptual space "like a sniper scout," projecting implications ahead and generating edge-condition hypotheses that Ti then weaves into arguments. It powers the cross-domain synthesis visible everywhere — politics read with the same tools as music trends, anomaly clustering, the combination of mythology with geology.

Under stress, Ne inverts into a paranoia generator, feeding the documented analysis paralysis: "conceptualize everything before acting."

## Ni — the leak

An INTP is not supposed to run this much introverted intuition. The 84% latent Ni is flagged as a "shadow strategist" that leaks mythic overfitting into the system: over-ascribing cosmic meaning to coincidence, weaving events into a fated grand narrative, feeling the "symbolic convergence" of a pattern before the evidence justifies it. This is the engine of the pattern-apophenia risk, and the corpus treats Dan's Law as the deliberately installed countermeasure — a formal procedure for stripping mythic gravity off genuine anomaly clusters.

## Si — memory and the trauma pattern

Tertiary introverted sensing maintains the vast high-fidelity archive — the minute recall of years-old details that makes the whole self-documentation project possible. It is also where the old betrayal pattern lives: memory of past betrayals (the 2004–05 parental rupture, the Alexis collapse) gets projected onto present situations and can take over his reactions without warning.

The **Ti-Si loop** is the type's signature pathology and is well-documented in the record: bypassing exploratory Ne to cycle between internal logic and past experience — replaying failures in exhaustive detail, rigid comfort-food ritual alongside broader physical neglect, sensory hypochondria.

## Fe — the feeling function, weak but craved

Inferior extraverted feeling is the weakest point and the most consequential. The need it represents — connection, emotional truth, validation — is desperately real, but the function itself is distrusted as irrational and deployed tactically, "like an actor uses makeup": high social intelligence, low emotional trust.

Documented consequences: rejection hypersensitivity that can turn a minor slight into withdrawal or explosive sarcasm; immature eruptions under stress; genuine blindness to hierarchy, reputation, and etiquette (identical irreverence toward authority and subordinates); and the "invisible genius" outcome — high output, low recognition, no networking instinct.

## The stack as one machine

Run together, the functions produce the behavioral record with uncomfortable precision: Ti supplies the standard of proof, Ne supplies the anomaly candidates, Si supplies the archive to check them against, Ni supplies the narrative gravity that must be actively resisted, and Fe's absence removes the social brake that would otherwise soften conclusions for company. That is the forensic default, derived rather than chosen.

The same arithmetic explains the costs: a mind that can prove things cannot stop litigating, and a memory that cannot forget keeps every wound current. The self-myth the stack sustains — the singular perceiver against a system too dull to see what he sees — also shows in what he chooses to consume: the great-man shelf of the 2024 Roman-Republic reading year (Caesar, Alexander, Oppenheimer, all tagged great-man-theory) and the Taxi Driver / King of Comedy self-portrait pair in his film canon pick out the same figure in history and cinema.

## What we still don't know

- **Function scores are AI-inferred from corpus behavior**, not from a proctored instrument. The Ni measurement in particular has no independent confirmation.
- **No data on how the stack presents post-closure.** All sources pre-date June 2026.
- **The earliest self-typing is a July 2013 message**: "as an INTP and heavily introverted, logic-based dude... I can't see myself flourishing in AA/NA" — used there to explain a rejection of group-based recovery in favor of self-directed reflection. It predates the personality models by a decade and is genuinely primary.
