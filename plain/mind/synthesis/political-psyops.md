# Political Psyops & Hybrid Events

**The short version.** Dan's political skepticism is not about picking sides. He is a democratic socialist who has zero loyalty to Donald Trump or the MAGA movement, and he uses that lack of tribal allegiance as credibility when he argues for unusual conclusions. His approach treats politics as both an all-consuming ideological pursuit and a kind of spectator sport — and the clearest example of his method at work is his analysis of January 6th, which he reads as a genuine riot that was also deliberately exploited by intelligence operatives to produce damning images of the populist right.

## The January 6th argument

The claim is deliberately narrow. Dan is not saying the rally was staged or the event was fake. Instead, he argues that a small operational insertion rode on top of organic conditions to manufacture a specific visual product: images of martyrdom and revolutionary violence designed to poison the populist right's future electoral chances.

The case is built on an "anomaly set" — details that don't fit the official narrative when studied closely. There are four categories:

**Ballistics.** The shooting of Ashli Babbitt produced a non-exiting torso wound with contained internal bleeding rather than the arterial spray one would expect from Hollywood depictions. The shooter went unidentified for months.

**Procedure.** No perimeter was established after lethal force was used in a volatile scene. Medical intervention was cosmetic — unzipping a jacket rather than performing real first aid. Officers intermingled with the crowd rather than securing it. SWAT stepped aside at the critical choke point.

**Crowd behavior.** The group ignored an open door just feet from the Senate chamber while Mitt Romney was evacuated through it. Fight footage resembles what one analyst called a "sad mosh pit." Police arrested and then released the Proud Boy who smashed a window.

**Documentation.** The only clean footage of the shooting came from John Sullivan, an anti-Trump activist who happened to be positioned perfectly to capture it.

## The "Sandwich" theory

In a series of conversations with Google's Gemini AI on January 2, 2026, this analysis was formalized under the name "Operation Wildfall." The core mechanism is called a "Sandwich" operation: an organic outer layer (the riot) with an inserted lethal core (the Babbitt shooting) treated as a "narrative necessity." Babbitt is described as "a prop" — unarmed, small, a white female Air Force veteran, cast for the specific optics of tragedy.

The surrounding doctrine is called "Inverted Containment": the outer perimeter is kept porous while the inner hard point is sacrificed, so the imagery is captured cleanly. Much of the supporting detail pile is acknowledged as a "Gish Gallop" — a flood of individually weak claims — and genuine stress-freeze trauma responses. But the surviving flags are the perfect framing, the textbook protocol violations, and the instant-available footage, especially when contrasted with footage that was suppressed.

The meta-point survives scrutiny either way, in Dan's reading: the point was the imagery, and the execution didn't need to be perfect, because almost nobody studies raw footage streams, and those who do are often dismissed as fringe.

In a separate conversation with Claude, an initially skeptical AI conceded that the coordination problem is overstated for a small group, called the procedural anomalies the strongest argument, rated it "the most internally coherent version of January 6th revisionism," and updated its assessment to "warrants genuine forensic investigation that has not occurred."

**A note on timing:** the Gemini "Operation Wildfall" codification is dated January 2, 2026, but the thesis itself is older. A ChatGPT session from June 15, 2025 — seven months earlier, on a different platform — has Dan independently posing the same question ("what are the odds the Babbitt shooting is a legitimate shooting... and not part of a false flag to build a narrative") and landing on the same hybrid verdict. The recurrence across platforms and months is itself evidence of how settled this conclusion was long before it got its formal vocabulary.

## The broader political picture

The January 6th conclusion generalizes into a structural-decay narrative: the Trump era as zero-sum team sport decided by one man's short-term interest; Democratic complicity through corporate capture and the marginalization of economic populists; Bernie Sanders' 2016 campaign as the definitive lost opportunity; identity liberalism versus the class-materialist left; and Chinese ascendancy against American self-sabotage.

All of it is consistent with a documented personal trajectory: Republican household, exit from the party in 2006, hard-left turn in 2019 through Bernie Sanders and the "dirtbag left" media ecosystem, with politics as primary entertainment.

**A prediction worth testing:** this trajectory may be cohort-driven rather than idiosyncratic. Two of Dan's named close political contacts — childhood friends from Uniontown, Pennsylvania — are independently documented as arriving at the same reluctant Trump fascination starting from Bernie-aligned positions. That three people from the same social graph, all starting from the same 2016-era economic-populist enthusiasm, converged on the same downstream fascination suggests the pattern belongs to a specific Rust Belt millennial cohort disillusioned by the Democratic establishment's handling of Sanders, not to something unique in Dan's own psychology.

The analytical engine underneath all of this is what the wiki calls "Dan's Law": when a coincidence cluster — crowd dynamics, procedural failures, informant presence, optical perfection — requires every element to be innocent simultaneously, treat it as signal and filter out the noise.

## Politics as aesthetic object

Alongside the forensic track runs an explicitly aesthetic one, and the two coexist rather than compete. Dan's own summary: politics is "primary entertainment plus near-total ideological pursuit, explicitly aesthetic/spectacle — I can't tell you how much fun I'm having with it."

The January 6th reading depends on both tracks at once. *That* the imagery was engineered is established procedurally. *Why* the imagery works — martyrdom for one side, revolutionary violence for the other, "too perfect" — is an aesthetic judgment. The whole Trump era is read this way: a "zero-sum binary team sport" whose lines are drawn by "one man's short-term selfish interest."

This dual register is what lets a committed democratic socialist analyze right-coded revisionism without tribal investment. The spectacle is a text to be read with the same tools used on music trends or workplace power moves.

## What would prove this wrong

1. **A clean official record.** If the full January 6th footage and procedural records were released and showed no anomalies — no protocol violations, no unexplained shooter identity, no open doors — the hybrid-event reading would collapse.
2. **A partisan pattern in the anomalies.** If the anomalies were shown to favor one party's narrative over another in a way that tracked with Dan's own politics, the "non-partisan forensic" claim would be weakened.
3. **A different cohort arriving at different conclusions.** If the same Rust Belt millennial cohort that Dan belongs to is shown arriving at pro-Trump or pro-establishment conclusions at the same rate, the "cohort pattern" reading would need revision.

## What we still don't know

- The full January 6th footage has not been publicly released. The analysis depends on what is available, and the suppressed material could change the picture.
- Whether the "Operation Wildfall" framing is Dan's own construction or was shaped by the AI systems he used to develop it is not resolved.
- The predictive claim — that the populist right was deliberately damaged by engineered imagery — has not been tested against subsequent electoral outcomes.
