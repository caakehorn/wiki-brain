# OCEAN Cross-Validation — Independent Behavioral Test

The Wiki Brain's working OCEAN estimate is **O ≈ 96 / C ≈ 15 / E ≈ 5 / A ≈ 5 / N ≈ 88**. This page records the cross-validation pass against independent material in the Mind wing rather than treating the original Big30 battery as sufficient evidence by itself.

The result is unusually coherent. Four of the five dimensions are strongly corroborated by independent behavioral residue and convergent models. Conscientiousness remains the only live internal tension, and that tension was already present in the source material rather than introduced by this synthesis.

## Working trait vector

| Trait | Estimate | Corroboration | Status |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Openness** | **96** | High; multi-source synthesis | Holds |
| **Conscientiousness** | **15** | High for generic C; persistence tension | Holds with caveat |
| **Extraversion** | **5** | Extremely high; quantitative residue | Holds |
| **Agreeableness** | **5** | High; facets + corpus nuance | Holds |
| **Neuroticism** | **88** | High; multiple independent mechanisms | Holds |

The resulting configuration is therefore **extremely high Openness / low Conscientiousness / floor-level Extraversion / floor-level Agreeableness / high Neuroticism**.

## 1. Extraversion ≈ 5 — strongest corroboration

This is the most robust dimension in the synthesis because it has behavioral residue that does not depend on a personality questionnaire.

**Contact architecture.** Lifetime inbound contact concentration is measured at **Gini 0.9601 across 496 handles**, with roughly **70% of volume carried by the top five handles**. Two-sided analysis finds the same concentration architecture in messages sent as received. This is residue rather than self-report: the social graph itself is extremely narrow and concentrated. [[wiki/mind/concepts/contact-gini]]

**5w4 sx/sp.** The Enneagram profile describes the social instinct as functionally absent and the operating pattern as **fusion-or-fortress**: either an unusually intense channel clears the admission barrier or the system retreats into solitude. That predicts tiny social bandwidth without predicting low interpersonal intensity. [[wiki/mind/profile/enneagram-5w4]]

**INTP / Fe-inferior.** The cognitive profile independently describes weak conventional social modulation, low sensitivity to hierarchy and reputation, and difficulty treating ordinary social expectations as authoritative. [[wiki/mind/profile/intp]]

**Deviance mapping.** The deviance audit places social aversion at an extreme level, providing another independent convergence point. [[wiki/mind/profile/deviance-mapping]]

**Verdict:** **E ≈ 5 is a floor-level finding.** The precise behavioral formulation is not simply “shy” or “socially anxious.” It is **very low social breadth combined with potentially extreme depth/intensity in a small number of admitted channels**.

## 2. Agreeableness ≈ 5 — strong, with an instrumental/affective split

The low-A reading is supported by the facet cluster: **Altruism 1, Trust 9, Sympathy 6, Submissiveness 1, Assertiveness 5, Modesty 5**. [[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]]

The important correction is that low Agreeableness does not mean uniformly low prosocial behavior. The corpus audit distinguishes **instrumental/provision behavior** from **affective sympathy**. Instrumental generosity can run above baseline even while affective sympathy remains low. The documented family transfer is an example where a literal Altruism-1 interpretation predicts badly.

The same structure appears in **vertical-authority skepticism**: other people's needs, status, expectations, or claims do not automatically acquire normative authority merely because they are socially conventional. Trust is particularly important because it is the facet with the strongest independent corpus confirmation in the existing audit. [[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]]

**Verdict:** **A ≈ 5 holds.** The correct description is **extremely low interpersonal compliance/affiliation/trust**, not “incapable of helping people.” The system can be materially generous without being sentimentally affiliative.

## 3. Neuroticism ≈ 88 — high reactivity behind an elaborate cognitive defense

The numerical foundation is strong: **Impulsiveness 96, Self-Consciousness 91, Vulnerability 78, Anxiety 67**. [[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]]

Independent Mind-wing material adds a mechanism rather than merely repeating the scores: the deviance audit places the intellect/impulsivity paradox around **95/100** and emotional processing around **85**; the cognitive architecture describes a Ti-dominant system capable of converting affect into recursive analysis; the Ti-Si loop and attachment/conflict pages document chronic threat-monitoring and catastrophic interpretation under relational threat; and Self-Consciousness has an independent corpus correlate in heightened self-monitoring.

The resulting phenotype is unusual: **high emotional reactivity plus unusually strong intellectual defenses against that reactivity**. The system does not merely experience an emotion; it immediately attempts to model it, explain it, predict it, and sometimes thereby intensifies it.

**Verdict:** **N ≈ 88 holds.** The important qualifier is that the high-N expression is heavily intellectualized rather than simply overtly emotional.

## 4. Openness ≈ 96 — strongest synthesis rather than single-test score

There is no direct O score in the existing Big30 table, so **O ≈ 96 is a synthesis**, not a reported instrument result. That distinction remains important. [[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]]

The convergence is nevertheless unusually strong: **Intellect 95, Liberalism 91, Introspection 87, Artistic Interests 81**, extreme cross-domain abstraction and synthesis, hyperfixation, symbolic and mythic construction, attraction to edge cases and conceptual boundary conditions, recursive self-analysis, creation of elaborate external cognitive systems including the Wiki Brain, and linguistic/cognitive deviance at the extreme end of the existing audit.

The 5w4 architecture supplies the component the facet table under-represents: the **4 wing turns difference into identity and aesthetic meaning**. Knowledge is not merely acquired for competence; it becomes material for constructing a distinct self-model and a private ontology. [[wiki/mind/profile/enneagram-5w4]]

The useful distinction is that **intellectual Openness appears even more extreme than conventional aesthetic Openness**. The profile is not simply omnivorous in taste. Its deeper signature is compulsive movement toward abstraction, novelty, synthesis, symbolic significance, and increasingly precise models.

**Verdict:** **O ≈ 96 is defensible and robust as a synthesis**, while remaining less instrument-clean than E, A, or N.

## 5. Conscientiousness ≈ 15 — the genuine unresolved dimension

Conscientiousness is the only trait where independent evidence points in two directions at once.

### Evidence for low generic C

- **Impulsiveness 96**
- SLOAN **Unstructured**
- schedule resistance
- external disorder alongside elaborate internal logical structure
- burst/collapse work patterns
- difficulty translating insight into stable behavior
- documented diagnosis-to-behavior gap

These are genuine Conscientiousness indicators in the conventional Big Five sense. [[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]]

### Evidence for a high persistence engine

At the same time, the Mind wing contains evidence for unusually strong persistence when a sufficiently meaningful **self-originated objective** is installed: the acquisition-drive page records a claim of approximately **95th-percentile or higher resilience for the goal**; long tenures such as **41 months at Au Za'atar** and **43 months at Nemacolin** coexist with the otherwise low-C profile; some self-originated projects run to completion despite substantial cost; and the same engine has produced the Wiki Brain, forensic tooling, procurement architectures, and quantitative corpus analyses.

The key problem is that **persistence toward self-selected objectives is not the same construct as generic Big Five Conscientiousness**. The existing Big30 facet table has no dedicated persistence dimension, so it can register the external manifestation—impulsivity, disorder, inconsistency—without measuring the motivational architecture that occasionally drives sustained completion. [[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]]

The corpus also contains genuine stalled self-set projects, so the persistence engine must not be romanticized into “actually highly conscientious.” It is **conditional, narrow, and unreliable outside sufficiently compelling objectives**.

### Resolution

> **Low bureaucratic/routine/external Conscientiousness + potentially very high self-originated persistence under high subjective objective-value.**

That is not a contradiction in the OCEAN estimate. It is a warning about what the C dimension is and is not measuring.

**Verdict:** **C ≈ 15 remains the correct estimate for standard Big Five Conscientiousness**, with the persistence engine explicitly tracked as an orthogonal mechanism rather than silently folded into C.

## Overall cross-validation result

The independent Mind-wing material does not merely repeat the original profile. It supplies different kinds of evidence that converge on the same geometry:

**O 96** — abstraction, novelty, introspection, symbolic/aesthetic differentiation, hyperfixation, recursive synthesis.

**C 15** — low routine regulation and external structure, with a separate and conditional self-directed completion engine.

**E 5** — extreme social narrowing quantitatively visible in the contact graph, reinforced by the sx/sp and social-blind architecture.

**A 5** — extreme independence, low trust/compliance/affective affiliation, with instrumental generosity preserved as a separate behavior.

**N 88** — high reactivity, self-monitoring, threat sensitivity and vulnerability, heavily mediated through intellectualization.

The important methodological result is that **the profile survives cross-validation precisely because the supporting evidence is not all of one kind**. Self-report facets, corpus residue, relationship architecture, cognitive-function analysis, Enneagram mechanics, and deviance measurements independently point toward the same trait geometry.

The strongest findings are **E ≈ 5, A ≈ 5, and N ≈ 88** because their supporting mechanisms are comparatively direct. **O ≈ 96** is a high-confidence synthesis rather than a direct test result. **C ≈ 15** is the least semantically simple dimension because generic conscientiousness and self-originated persistence appear partially decoupled.

## The resulting model

The profile is therefore best understood not as “high openness and low everything else,” but as a highly asymmetric motivational/cognitive architecture:

**abstraction + introspection + novelty + intensity + autonomy**

are strongly amplified, while

**social breadth + interpersonal compliance + routine regulation + generalized trust**

are strongly suppressed.

The unusual part is that **drive is not suppressed in parallel with structure**. A compelling internally authored objective can temporarily recruit extraordinary persistence without producing the ordinary behavioral package associated with high Conscientiousness.

That distinction should remain attached to every future OCEAN interpretation. Otherwise the model will make the same category error already identified in the Big30 page: treating a missing brake as evidence that there is no engine.

## Methodological status

This is a **cross-validation synthesis**, not a new psychometric administration. The underlying Big30 instrument lacks a documented instrument name, norm group, administration date, and version. The O estimate is inferred because the source battery does not directly report Openness. The corpus itself can validate behavioral correlates but cannot retroactively establish that every psychometric construct has been measured cleanly.

The appropriate status is therefore **robust working model, not clinical measurement**.
