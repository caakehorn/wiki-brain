# Twitter / X — 2016

Periscope NYE bedroom DJ sets on day one, then a thin year of asides. Public posting is a valve, not a diary; the real record of 2016 is elsewhere in the corpus.

## Narrative

**A thin year on the account and a busy one in life — which is itself the
finding.** Seventy-six originals, and almost none of them about the things the
rest of the corpus says were happening. Public posting in 2016 is a valve, not a
diary.

**The tweet this page exists to hold is 7 February.** ***"Had lit times playing
@ulmdub birthday party last night at OSU"*** (19:34 UTC). For months
[[wiki/interests/music/overview]] could not tell whether
`raw/self/dox-scan/FULL TWITTER ANALYSIS.txt` had invented that line or whether
the archive's 2016 was incomplete, and leaned — wrongly — toward the first. The
backend fetch of 2026-09-02 returned the tweet and that page has since recorded
the correction in full. What this page adds is a second, independent reason to
believe the diagnosis: **the operator spreadsheet's 2014–2024 rows sit at a mean
position-in-month of 0.492 against 0.500 for a uniform draw**, so the spreadsheet
is not query-shaped the way the pre-2014 scrape is. Its failure on this row was
not systematic under-sampling; it simply does not have every row. That is a
weaker and more awkward defect than a measurable bias, because there is no
statistic that will tell you where the next missing one is.

**The year opens and closes on broadcast.** Two Periscope streams at 00:45 and
00:49 UTC on 1 January — *"NYE bedroom DJ set"* — then a third on 20 January from
a Pittsburgh snowstorm. After the DJ streams stop, the same instinct reappears
pointed at news: on **21 April**, *"@DKaplanWTAE car chase in uniontown went
through my yard. Here's the hill where he went airborne"* — a first-hand tip,
with footage, to a television reporter. That is the Sandy 2012 behaviour at local
scale, and it recurs at national scale in 2023.
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/millennial-digital-witness]] should carry all three.

**Golf becomes real.** *"Nine iron"* (1 March), a WGT Golf clip from a virtual
Congressional (10 June), and then the actual thing: **he attended the 2016 U.S.
Open at Oakmont**, posting from the course across 13–16 June including the rain
delay — *"Oakmont rough like backyard of a foreclosure house"*, *"Oakmont greens
on some god-mode shit"*, *"We out here @ 2016 U.S. Open Golf Championship"*. See
[[wiki/interests/golf]] and [[wiki/interests/concert-record/index]] for the
attended-event record.

**Instruments arrive by inheritance, not purchase.** On 6 March: *"I just
found/inherited an alto sax, clarinet and mandolin. The amount of time I'm gonna
spend becoming mediocre at them will be crzy"*, and the next day *"Got a small
fortune in poorly maintained instruments here"*. Ten years later the 2025 record
has him teaching himself piano. The pattern is consistent and worth naming: he
acquires instruments and teaches himself, and he says in advance that he expects
to be mediocre.

**And 2016 is not apolitical, which matters for the counts.** He is against
Trump (*"Voting for trump is like not getting car u want and instead driving ur
own car into a wall with ur family & country in it"*, 19 October), against the
Democratic convention's staging (*"The crowd signs at DNC are weirding me the
fuck out"*, 26 July), and against the general condition (*"Disgusting that
compromised values are the norm"*). Any political-share figure for 2016 computed
from a fixed keyword list will under-report this year badly, because none of
those four posts uses a term a 2020s political vocabulary would look for.

Employment leaves one trace: *"I think the people at my job training are hired
actors carrying out an elaborate and gloriously stupid performance art piece"*
(24 February).

> **A strand of this year is not narrated here**, for the same reason as 2015 and
> on the same terms: the standing directive in `CLAUDE.md`. The transcript is
> unchanged and nothing has been removed; the narrative simply does not build on
> it.
