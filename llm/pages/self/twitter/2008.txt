# Twitter / X — 2008

The account's first year, recovered only on 2026-09-02 and thirteen months earlier than the archive previously reached. Full Sail student life at close range — orientation, 7am labs, the algebra requirement, the December final — plus a first makeshift studio, emo and pop-punk at saturation, and fourteen hours of World of Warcraft to close the year.

## Narrative

**The account's first year, and it did not exist in this wiki until 2026-09-02.**
The archive previously began on 2009-10-20; a backend fetch recovered 213 earlier
tweets, 95 of them from 2008.

The account opens on **24 September 2008** with *"i fucking looove winter park.
this town is unreal."* Winter Park is where Full Sail is. The account is not a
diary someone started and later pointed at a new life — it **begins at the
arrival**, and the second tweet, forty-five minutes later, says what the arrival
was for: *"Really feels like life has started over now. It's weird enjoying life
again. So stoked."* Read against [[wiki/timeline/periods/dark-era-2007-2008]],
that is Dan naming an exit from a period on the day the public record starts.

What follows is student life at close range. Orientation on 22 October, then the
7am starts (*"getting accustomed to getting up this early. i'll be doing it for a
month evvvvvery day. FUCK."*), twenty-hour class days, the labs, the maths
requirement he resents (*"why am i learning fucking ALGEBRA again. gb2 fourth
grade."*, and the folk claim *"Fact 90% of full sail students fail math"*), and
the final on 18 December — *"Killed it. Packing to go home :("*.

Three other threads run through it. **Gear and a first studio**: a Korg
padKONTROL on 8 October, and on 12 November *"Living room is now a makeshift
studio"*. **Emo and pop-punk at saturation** — All Time Low, The Maine, Cobra
Starship, Hit The Lights, Forever The Sickest Kids, Panic, The Starting Line,
Fall Out Boy at the House of Blues on 12 December, and Hey Monday twice,
including flying back to Orlando early on 28 December to catch them. And **World
of Warcraft**, closing the year on 31 December with *"in the past 24 hours, i've
played 14 hours of world of warcraft."* The WoW habit is dated from both ends:
on 2011-12-16 he reports hitting level cap after *"6 years of (intermittent) wow
playing"*, which puts the start around 2005–06, three years before this page
opens.

Pennsylvania is already the place he does not want to be: *"I don't want to go to
cold, lame PA"* and *"i fucking hate going home"*, both 19 December. That
polarity — Florida and later New York as escape, Pennsylvania as gravity — is set
in the account's first ninety days and holds for a decade, until
*"Au revoir Pennsylvania. Adiós Trump country."* on 2019-03-03.

**One thing this year is evidence of that no page could see before 2026-09-02:**
the voice arrives finished. Lowercase, fragment, profane, self-mocking,
enthusiasm and contempt in the same sentence. There is no juvenile period in the
record that later matures — the register on 24 September 2008 is recognisably the
register of 2026. What changes over seventeen years is the subject matter and the
sentence length, not the person doing the talking.

## Gaps

**This page is a lower bound, and the shortfall is structural.** The fetch that
produced it was search-based and capped at ten results per query. Every month
that returned ten hits is truncated at ten: **October, November and December
2008 are all incomplete.** Only 24 September is a strong candidate for a
complete day, at six tweets.

The account's creation datetime was **not** retrievable — the backend user
lookup returned an id (`16430736`) but no `created_at`. Creation on 24
September 2008 is an inference from three consistent facts: the earliest tweet
retrieved, two queries before it returning nothing, and the
`#MyTwitterAnniversary` tweet of 2022-09-24. It is not a recorded creation
date, and should not be cited as one.

A complete X account export would close both gaps.
