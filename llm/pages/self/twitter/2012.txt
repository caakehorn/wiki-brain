# Twitter / X — 2012

O&A internship applications (@erockradio, @notsam), Halo 4 tray errors, election-night skepticism, and the Pro Tools-cert-in-NYC self-pitch. The public record of wanting into that radio world is here in two near-identical tweets sent four minutes apart. Live-scrape; incomplete.

## Narrative

**The year the public record shows him trying to get in, and the year it shows
what he does when a disaster is happening in front of him.**

**Hurricane Sandy is not a tweet on this page; it is an operation.** Across 29
and 30 October he posts a liveblog URL six times — *"#Sandy pics from NYC on the
liveblog gogo"*, *"#sandy liveblog updated. pics/gifs/nyc news"*, *"Deteriorating
conditions and all kinds of bad shit"* — while feeding sourced material to the
Opie & Anthony hosts by name: the 50-house six-alarm fire in Breezy Point to
@OpieRadio, the submerged HMS Bounty, the NYPD 911 call volumes (*"Total calls
for the day since midnight: 8,362, Total jobs in the queue as of 5:30am:
4,807"*), the MTA official conceding flooding *"worse than their predicted worst
case scenario"*. He also files first-hand observation: *"Manhattan is straight
empty. No cabs. No stores. No people."* And then, at 12:25 on 30 October, he
names what he has been doing: ***"Sandy is like first true 'digital disaster'.
Can't remember so much user generated content from a storm."***
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/millennial-digital-witness]] cites that last line as the
moment of noticing. It is better than that — he noticed it because he was one of
the people generating it, and the aggregating, sourcing and publishing are all in
the same forty-eight hours.

**The bid for the radio world is on the record twice, four minutes apart.** On 18
November, to @notsam and then to @erockradio: *"what's the process like to get an
O&A internship? I'm a recent music engineering grad with pro tools certification
living in NYC."* Two hours later: *"I just sent in an app."* Nothing came of it.
See [[wiki/interests/opie-and-anthony]].

**And there is a second, quieter ambition that also goes nowhere.** On 27 August:
*"@adamlucidi let's talk soon, been tossing around some ideas for a podcast and i
want to see if I can't get you involved."* The day after, *"I blogged about a
world without internet"* — there was a blog. Fourteen years before the AI
video-essay project of [[wiki/work/tech/ai-video-essays]], the shape of the
ambition is already podcast-and-blog, and the outcome is already no artifact.

**The 2012 politics are not liberal, and they are not yet left.** On 3 January,
of the Obama administration: *"The one thing I've taken away from the last 3
years: no we can't."* On election night, love with a knife in it: *"obama going
off the cuff in Iowa tonight. LOVE THIS GUY (except those pesky drone strikes)"*.
And the next morning, a four-item platform in his own words: ***"Let's do this
Barry: end corporate welfare. establish congressional term limits. stop DEA raids
of marijuana vendors. GTFO afghanistan etc"*** (7 November). Anti-corporate,
anti-war, anti-drug-war, anti-incumbency. [[wiki/mind/synthesis/2020-left-turn]]
treats 2020 as the arrival of an ideology; this is the substrate it arrived on,
and it is populist rather than progressive. He also refuses the ratings
narrative on the night — *"I refuse to believe that tonight's earlier results
were as close as we were told. Need to keep it tight for ratings."*

**The media diet that the pipeline arguments depend on is documented here.** On
23 March: *"@joerogan still planning to have Sam Harris on the podcast? Super
excited for that."* In August, two posts about Duncan Trussell on the Joe Rogan
Experience, one calling an episode *"the new high water mark for JRE"*. Alongside
the daily O&A intake and a *"I don't get down with o&a right-wing ideology,
but..."* on 26 February. The 2020 conversion story and the 2022 account of being
*radicalized away* from that tribe both need this year to be true, and it is.

**Two moves in the physical record.** *"Goodbye, Brooklyn!"* on 29 March,
*"Hello, Manhattan."* on 30 March — the Brooklyn-to-Manhattan move dated to the
day, against a wiki that had it in May. And by autumn he is working retail:
*"every South American I meet at The Gap"* (29 September), *"Sometimes I write my
own stories about the cartoons on the fitting room walls at work"* (30
September).

**Finally, the cohort.** On 23 February: *"People who need to come visit me:
@danpolyak @EricJester @JoshBrannan @Woodguts"*. It is the only place in the
corpus where Dan enumerates his own core friend group, and four names is the
whole list. Three have pages — [[wiki/people/dan-polyak]],
[[wiki/people/eric-jester]], [[wiki/people/josh-brannan]] — and the fourth,
@Woodguts, is still unidentified after 17 posts spanning 2009 to 2013.

## Gaps

**The count on this page is 205 and the year's true total is unknown and higher.**
Every row is `live-x-scrape`; the operator spreadsheet does not begin until
2013-08-17.

Monthly counts: 27, 20, 19, 10, 20, 10, 10, 19, 20, 20, 26, 4. **December 2012 holds
four rows and is the worst-covered month of the year** — the Sandy material in
October survives only because he posted through it in volume.

> **The shape of this defect, stated once and referenced from every affected
> page.** The pre-August-2013 rows were retrieved by search backends capped at ten
> results per query, and a capped query does not sample a month — it returns the
> month's *end*. Mean position-in-month is **0.838** for the 2009 backfill and
> **0.681** for the 2010–2013 scrape, against **0.500** for a uniform draw and
> **0.492** for the operator spreadsheet's own 2014–2024 rows. Monthly counts here
> are therefore near-multiples of ten because they record how many queries were
> run. Full account on [[wiki/self/twitter]].
