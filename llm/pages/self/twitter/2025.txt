# Twitter / X — 2025

Collapse-year quiet. Piano self-teaching, #songs2die4, the memoir-title joke 'Other Things, and Other Things Other than That.' Thirteen originals in the spreadsheet; the life is happening in texts, not tweets.

## Narrative

**Thirteen originals, the lowest count in the archive, and the year the account
stops being where his life is recorded.** The iMessage corpus and the rest of the
wiki carry 2025; this page carries a residue.

What is in the residue is worth naming, because it is unusually consistent. **Six of
the thirteen are political and five of those are about competence rather than
ideology** — the Hegseth confirmation hearing (*"Democratic Senators offered few, if
any questions of substance. Republicans used their time to grandstand"*, 14 January),
a geography question a defence nominee could not answer, foreign-aid conditionality
as a position he genuinely cannot model (*"one of the only takes that exists across
the spectrum as a legitimate, 'reasonable' policy position that I truly can't
understand how or why anyone could possibly think this is a good idea"*, 5 January).
The 2020–2023 register — ideology, faction, definitional discipline — has narrowed to
*is anyone in this room doing their job*.

**And one of the thirteen is the digital-witness reflex, still firing.** On 1
February, after the Philadelphia medevac crash, three posts in five hours checking
whether the national press had covered it: *"So NYT & WaPo still have zero coverage of
the Philly crash story. Wild."* → *"Update: 3 hours later and still nothing. A plane
fell out of the sky onto a residential neighborhood in a major city and…noting?"* →
*"Nothing? @nyt @washingtonpost"*. Monitoring coverage and timing the gap is the same
act as the Sandy liveblog of 2012 and the WTAE tip of 2016, reduced to its smallest
possible form.

**Music returns, in the least professional way available.** 7 January: *"My
self-teaching piano lesson tonight is figuring out a bunch songs from criminally
under-appreciated national treasure @NickLutsko."* Ten years after the SLOPPP
operation ended and nine years after he inherited a box of instruments and predicted
he would become mediocre at them, the practice is a man teaching himself somebody
else's songs at night — nine likes, the highest of the year. In October the
`#songs2die4` tag appears, which is what carries forward into 2026.

The one line people quote is 27 February: *"Now that I have my title I'm ready to
write my memoir 'Other Things, and Other Things Other than That'"* — his third
memoir-title joke on this account, after the two of 29 December 2017 and *"GO READ A
BOOK OR SOMETHING"* of 5 December 2014. Three titles, no memoir, eleven years.

## Gaps

**Nine of twelve months hold no rows**, and the whole second half of the year is
represented by a single post on 2 October. Thirteen originals is what the export
contains, not what the account posted.

Other pages in this wiki describe 2025 as a collapse-year silence and reason from the
count. **Treat it as a lower bound.** The true 2025 total is the single most valuable
number an official X Settings export could return, and until one exists the silence
is unverified rather than established.
