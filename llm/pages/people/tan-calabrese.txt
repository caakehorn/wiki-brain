## The Angelfire incident

No date is given beyond childhood/adolescence, predating the earliest corpus contact by years.

**The friendship recovered.** Whatever the rupture cost at the time, it was not permanent — Tan reappears as an active corpus contact from February 2016, five years (at least) after the incident, through May 2020.
