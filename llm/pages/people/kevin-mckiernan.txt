# Kevin McKiernan

Kevin McKiernan was Dan's employer — company president at
[[wiki/work/creative-license|Creative License]], a small production-
services company at 71 8th Ave in the West Village — for roughly the
first half of Dan's post-Full-Sail stretch in New York, from January 2011
until sometime in 2012 (the exact end date is contradicted between
sources; see the employer page). He is the only person named in
connection with this job anywhere in the corpus, and he is also, in an
odd way, the corpus's thinnest well-attested figure: the wiki's AI
dossiers assign him a load-bearing role — the antagonist in the founding
instance of Dan's entire vertical-authority-skepticism architecture — on
the strength of essentially no primary evidence about him personally.

## What is actually documented

Dan's resume lists the job's duties directly under McKiernan's
supervision: daily executive-assistant tasks, travel logistics, calendar
management, "personal project requests," graphic-design work for the
company's website and business development, and — notably, given what
comes next — "oversight of office and employee expenses." Beyond the job
description, the only primary trace of McKiernan himself is a single
Facebook message he sent Dan on **December 3, 2011**: "check your phone
pls." No reply survives in the export, and nothing about the message's
content indicates whether it was work-related or personal. McKiernan's
contact card appears in five separate snapshots of Dan's Facebook address
book spanning January 2016 through October 2021, consistently carrying
the email `kevin@creativelicense.com` and, in most instances, the number
`+1 917-549-6163` (one 2018 snapshot instead lists `+1 312-651-6898`).
Dan kept this contact for close to a decade after the job that connects
them ended.

## The allegation the dossiers carry, and its evidentiary status

`THE_DAN_FRANK_BOOTLOADER.md` and `THE_DAN_FRANK_MANUAL.md` — AI-authored
synthesis documents, not primary records — both state that Dan "exits
Creative License in February 2012 over airfare-billing discrepancies and
altered intern contracts," language repeated near-verbatim across both
files. Neither document names McKiernan as the specific person responsible
for the alleged billing fraud or contract alterations, though he is the
only person in the corpus with the authority to be — company president,
Dan's direct supervisor, the one figure the job's "oversight of...
expenses" duty would have put Dan in position to audit.

No message, email, financial record, or contemporaneous first-person
account describing the alleged fraud, its discovery, or McKiernan's
specific conduct was located anywhere in `raw/` for this page. The claim
exists solely inside the two dossiers, which restate it in nearly
identical phrasing — evidence they share a common source or that one
copied the other, not that two independent accounts corroborate it (the
same non-independence pattern found and flagged on
[[wiki/mind/psychosexual/taboo-and-boundary-testing]] for an unrelated
claim). Full discussion of the date conflict and the missing primary
support is on [[wiki/work/creative-license]] rather than duplicated here;
this page's job is to be honest about what it can and cannot say about
the man himself, which is: he existed, he employed Dan, he sent one
documented text asking Dan to check his phone, and Dan kept his number
for years afterward. Everything else attached to his name in this corpus
is a role assigned to him by a narrative written years later, by a model,
without his side ever being asked.

## Gaps

Nothing beyond the above is documented about McKiernan as a person — no
age, no other employees, no account of what Creative License actually
produced, no independent confirmation he is even still reachable at the
address-book contact information. Whether the December 2011 message
relates to the alleged billing dispute or is unrelated is unknown. If a
primary source ever surfaces describing the actual circumstances of the
job's end, it should replace the current framing rather than supplement
it, since — as on the employer page — there is currently nothing
first-hand to supplement.
