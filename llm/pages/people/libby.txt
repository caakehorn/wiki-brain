# Libby

Libby was an elderly Manhattan woman for whom [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]
worked as a paid personal assistant and informal carer through **2024** — driving
her to hospital and doctor's appointments, taking her food, packing her
apartment, handling her correspondence, and being paid in cash and cheques at
rates that reached **$500 for a little over three hours**. Dan never met her. She
exists in this corpus entirely through Annie's running commentary, 116 messages
of it, and she is the single best-documented thing about Annie's working life in
the whole archive.

She matters here for three reasons that have nothing to do with her. She is the
**largest documented income source on Annie's side of the relationship**; she
falsifies a standing claim this wiki has carried about Annie's employment; and
the arrangement ends, along with the money, immediately before the period the
wiki dates as the collapse.

> finances and household, recorded from one side of a private conversation she
> was not party to and could not correct. It is kept because it is load-bearing

## The identification, and its evidential status

**Libby is very probably Libby Titus — born Elizabeth Jurist — the singer and
songwriter, married to Donald Fagen of Steely Dan.** The identification is
inferred rather than stated, and it rests on two independent things in the record
rather than on any outside search.

The first is Dan's own. On **2024-03-17 at 16:40** Annie writes *"Did you know
Libby was in a movie with Jack Nicholson"*, and at **22:33 that same evening** Dan
sends a link — five times, in the burst pattern the corpus is full of — to a
Tumblr post titled `girlfriend-muse-libby-titus-elizabeth-jurist`. He looked her
up the same night and filed the answer into the thread. That is a
contemporaneous identification made by the subject of this wiki, not a
reconstruction by it.

The second is corroboration from inside the household. Annie reports a **Donald**
who lives with Libby and who told her, on 2024-05-28, *"that he was the first
person to ever use the term 'gaslighting' in a song"* — at which *"Libby screamed
'YEAH ABOUT ME YOU ASSHOLE!'"* Steely Dan's *Gaslighting Abbie* is on **Two
Against Nature** (2000), and Fagen married Libby Titus in 1993. Two unrelated
details — a Jack Nicholson film credit and a spouse with that specific songwriting
claim — converge on one person.

**What the page does not do is treat this as verified.** No message names her
surname; the Tumblr link is Dan's inference from Annie's anecdote, and Annie
never confirms it. Recorded as **inferred identification with two supports**, and
flagged as such in the infobox.

## The arc, February to December 2024

The relationship is bounded almost exactly by the calendar year: **115 of the 116
messages fall in 2024**, and one straggles into 2025. Inside that year it moves
through three clear phases.

**February — the trial.** It opens on **2024-02-08** with *"Libby asked me to come
again tomorrow lol"*, which implies at least one prior visit the corpus does not
hold. Within days the register is set and it does not change for a year: the work
and the money arrive in the same sentence. *"I am taking Libby food from
Antonuccis. So hopefully she will give me something for bringing her food"*
(02-11). *"I may try to see Libby tomorrow to get some money"* (02-13). *"I gotta
gotta gotta get up early tomorrow and help Libby. I need money."* (02-16).

**March — the illness, and the work becoming medical.** On **2024-03-05** Annie
reports that Libby has told her she has brain cancer, and *"Libby just said 'I
think I'm dying..'"*; the following day, that it is also back in her right lung.
From that point the job changes character. Annie is driving her to appointments
(03-10), sitting with her in waiting rooms (*"we are waiting to see doctor now"*,
03-11), taking her for blood tests before a scheduled procedure (03-17), and
spending half-hours on the phone with doctors between shifts (03-19). On
**03-20** Libby emails her **cemetery plots** — *"Libby just emailed me her
CEMETERY PLOTS?!"*

**May onward — the money, and the second job.** The rate becomes explicit.
**2024-05-17:** *"I was literally with Libby for just over 3 hours today.. she
paid me 500."* A week later she invites Dan to guess the figure for four hours.
By this point Annie is working both jobs — *"Now I'll be here 6 days a week and
then Libby in mornings"* (05-08) — and saying plainly what it is costing her:
*"I don't want to get worked again and pass out like last time."* The Libby money
is earmarked in the messages for specific household purchases, most often phones
and phone service (*"Well I am hoping now working for Libby again we can get
phones soon"*, 05-09).

## What it corrects

**The standing account of Annie's employment is wrong for 2024.** The AI
assessment in `raw/self/dox-md/Honest assessment and value judgment analysis.md`
states that Annie *"was fired in 2023 and spent a full year unemployed"*, and the
framing has propagated. The message record shows her **working two jobs through
mid-2024** — a six-day-a-week position plus mornings at Libby's — and earning, at
the documented rate, more per hour than anything on record for Dan in the same
period. Corrected on [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer]].

This is not a small revision. The unemployment claim has been doing work in the
corpus's account of the relationship's economics, in which Dan is the sole
provider and the money runs one way
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine]] records a net **$119K–$123K** outflow
from Dan to Annie across the decade). That total is unaffected — it is derived
from payment-app records, not from an employment assumption — but the picture of
2024 specifically is not what the wiki has been carrying.

## The texture, which is the part worth keeping

Almost nothing in this corpus records Annie doing something for somebody else
over a sustained period, without an audience, and the Libby material is the
largest such stretch in eleven years. It is also, unusually for this archive,
funny. Libby shows her a brooch and remarks *"I did cocaine once, but I didn't
like it"* (05-22). At a medical appointment she asks the woman at the front desk
where she can get her hat — it is a hijab — and on being told, replies *"oh with
my past I probably shouldn't"* (03-22). She fires her other helper (05-02), takes
against a replacement named Janine (05-10), gives Annie a pair of loafers (05-09),
and cc's her on so much correspondence that Annie ends up looking at a spreadsheet
of the household bank accounts and says she felt wrong doing it (05-30).

Annie's own register in these messages is worth noting against the rest of her
record: she is reliable, she turns up, she is trusted with money, medical
appointments and correspondence, and the person she is working for likes her.
None of that appears anywhere else in the corpus at this length.

## Gaps

1. **When and how it ended.** 115 messages in 2024, one in 2025, and no closing
   event anywhere. Did Libby die, move, or simply stop calling? The operator can
   almost certainly answer in a sentence, and it dates the start of Annie's
   income collapse.
2. **What the total came to.** Individual payments are visible ($500 for three
   hours; an unstated figure for four) but no running total exists. The Venmo and
   Cash App records that establish the Dan→Annie flow would show the other
   direction if they were swept for it.
3. **Whether the identification is right.** One sentence from the operator —
   he sent the Tumblr link — settles it. If wrong, the two supports above need
   another explanation and this page needs a correction rather than an edit.
4. **The other job.** *"I'll be here 6 days a week"* names a second 2024 employer
   that appears nowhere in `wiki/work/` or on Annie's page. Unidentified.
