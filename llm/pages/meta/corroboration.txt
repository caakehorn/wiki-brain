# Corroboration against the message record

**Generated by `bin/wiki-corroborate page`. Do not hand-edit — a hand
edit fails `bin/wiki-corroborate check` and is overwritten on the next
run.** This is the record of which dated claims in this wiki have been
checked against the 265,000 first-party messages in `raw/`, what came
back, and what it changed.

**It is evidence, not a claim.** It records that a check happened and
what it settled. What a corroboration *means* is synthesis, and
synthesis happens on an ordinary page.

Years absent from that table hold nothing at all. **A claim dated
outside coverage cannot be checked here and is not recorded as
unsupported** — `uncovered` and `absent` are different outcomes and the
tool refuses to let one stand in for the other.

## Outcomes

| Outcome | Records | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `absent` | 1 | the window was read and holds nothing about this claim |
| `contradicted` | 1 | the record disagrees with the page |
| `corroborated` | 0 | the record says what the page says |
| `extended` | 2 | the record holds material the page did not have |
| `refined` | 1 | the record narrows or sharpens it — a date fixed, an hour added |
| `uncovered` | 0 | the corpus does not reach this date — nothing could be checked |

## What this page cannot tell you

**It is opt-in, so an absent page is unexamined rather than settled.**
Counting these records as coverage of the wiki is the same category
error as reading [[wiki/meta/claim-validity]]'s counts as coverage of
its corpus.

**A cited row is a pointer, not a quotation.** The ledger stores
timestamps, directions and handles; the messages themselves stay in
`raw/` and are quoted on the pages that argue from them. Every
citation here was verified to exist in the archive at the moment it
was recorded.
