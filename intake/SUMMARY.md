# Intake ledger — summary

GENERATED by `bin/intake`, rewritten on every write. Do not hand-edit.
`intake/units.json` is the same data as JSON; `intake/events.jsonl` is the
append-only log both are derived from.

**Read the coverage figure before quoting a mean.** Every quantity below is
computed only from events that carry a number. Events logged without one are
real events — they count toward totals, timing and clustering — and they are
excluded from every quantity figure. A mean over some of the events must never
be cited as a mean over all of them.
