NFL TOPLINE

One game in, one verdict out. The worksheet does the arithmetic; Claude does the reading.

Read this once. This is a decision aid, not an edge. Sportsbooks price NFL games extremely well — if your projection differs from the market by less than about two points, that difference is noise, not information. The verdicts below are deliberately conservative and most honest reads come back Pass. Never stake money you would mind losing.

What the columns mean

    

      

        
Market %

        
The chance the sportsbook's price implies, after the house cut has been stripped out. This
          is what you're betting against — not the raw price.

      

      

        
Our %

        
The chance implied by Claude's projected margin and total, run through the historical
          scatter of NFL results (margins land about 13 points either side of the projection; totals
          about 10.5). This treats every margin as equally likely, which isn't quite true — 3 and 7
          come up far more often than neighbouring numbers, so trust this slightly less on spreads
          sitting exactly on a key number.

      

      

        
Edge

        
Our % minus Market %. Positive means we think the bet is underpriced. Anything under about
          four percentage points sits inside the model's own error bar — treat it as zero.

      

      

        
Hold

        
The book's built-in margin on that market. Standard is about 4.5% on a −110/−110 line. A
          higher hold means you need a bigger real edge just to break even.

      

      

        
Stake

        
Fractional Kelly: the mathematically growth-optimal bet size, cut down by the fraction you
          picked. Quarter Kelly is the sane default because your win probability is an estimate, not a fact.

      

      

        
Closing line value

        
The only honest scorekeeping. Write down the line you got. If it consistently moves in your
          favour before kickoff, you're finding something real. Win-loss over one season tells you nothing.

      

    


    
House rules worth keeping

    

      

        
One unit, always

        
Pick a stake size and hold it. Chasing a loss with a bigger bet is the single most reliable
          way to go broke.

      

      

        
Shop the number

        
Getting −2.5 instead of −3 matters more over a season than any read on this page. Three is
          the most common NFL margin by a wide distance.

      

      

        
Skip the parlay

        
Each leg carries the house cut and they compound. A four-leg parlay is a worse bet than any
          of its legs alone.

      

      

        
Log every bet

        
Date, game, market, line, price, stake, result. Without the log you cannot tell skill from
          a hot streak, and you will assume it was skill.
