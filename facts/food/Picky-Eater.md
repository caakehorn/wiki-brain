# Picky Eater

Dan has a highly restricted and consistent diet that he describes as eating "like a toddler."

## Common Foods

Primary staples include:

- chicken strips
- french fries
- candy
- fruit
- cheeseburgers
- tacos
- sandwiches

## Plain Food Preference

Dan strongly prefers foods completely plain.

Examples:

**Cheeseburger**
- meat
- cheese
- bread

**Taco**
- shell
- meat
- cheese

**Sandwich**
- meat
- cheese
- roll

Additional toppings, sauces, or ingredients are generally rejected.

## Accepted Vegetables

The only vegetables Dan reliably accepts are:

- corn
- lettuce

He refuses foods containing:

- onions
- tomatoes

## Pickles

Pickles are described as the ultimate food dealbreaker. This is not a mild dislike but an absolute refusal.

Any meal containing pickles or pickle flavoring is considered unacceptable.

## Relevance

This fact affects restaurant ordering, meals with others, travel, and daily food choices. It represents a stable long-term preference rather than a temporary dietary phase.
