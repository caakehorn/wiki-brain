# Ally Lubin (Alexandra Lubin)

Ally Lubin is a woman Dan has never met and has been in love with since
roughly 2008 — the longest-running attachment in the corpus that has no
physical component at all. He found her as an internet-famous figure in the
mid-2000s AbsolutePunk/Buzznet scene, spent a decade watching her from a
distance ("i've spent 10 years idolizing this girl i've never met as the
epitome of whatever 'cool' is"), paid a mutual friend $25 to introduce them
in December 2018, and in the autumn of 2019 turned the resulting friendship
into a transaction — several thousand dollars sent to her while she was
unemployed and suicidal, against photographs he mostly did not receive.
The relationship is real on both sides, it is funny on both sides, and its
governing fact is that it activates when Dan's primary bond fails. The most
recent contact in the corpus is nine unanswered messages sent on **June 1,
2026, nineteen hours and thirty-eight minutes after he ended his eleven-year
relationship with [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]**, one of which reads:
*"more importantly when am I allowed to come try to marry on you."*

She is from Boca Raton, Florida; she lived in New York City in the early
2010s; she has been in North Carolina since at least December 2018. She was
born June 26, 1990 — nineteen months younger than Dan, a fact he gets wrong
in the thread and she corrects (2019-09-01: *"you're way too old for me"* /
*"I'm literally 29 / You're older than me"*). The date is derived, not
stated: on 2020-06-08 she writes *"I turn 30 in 18 days,"* and she gives her
sign as Cancer. Her cats are Edgar and Sylvia. Her father is
Bob Lubin.

## The seventeen-year approach

The attachment predates any conversation by a decade, and Dan is explicit
about its origin. Writing to Polyak on December 7, 2018, hours after their
first real exchange, he sets the clock himself: *"i've spent 10 years
idolizing this girl i've never met as the epitome of whatever 'cool' is,
which i will include funny and pretty under that umbrella as well."* Ten
years back puts the start at 2008 — the year Ally herself independently
nominates in the thread as her own peak (*"I peaked in 2008"*), and the
period the wiki files as [[wiki/timeline/periods/dark-era-2007-2008|the dark
era]]. The venue was the AbsolutePunk.net / emo-scene internet: the Facebook
threads reference ap.net, its editor Jon Lally, Hunter Moore's IsAnyoneUp,
Model Mayhem, and "ALU" as a handle famous enough that Dan and Polyak riff
on it as an institution — *"i sent my transcripts to ALU, but got a
rejection letter in the mail."* This is the concrete instance of what
[[wiki/mind/concepts/the-cool-metric|the cool metric]] describes:
weaponized subcultural literacy, personified in one woman, at seventeen.

The Facebook takeout shows the approach as a slow, one-directional
accretion. Nine documented interactions from Dan to her content between
**May 20, 2011** and **August 15, 2013** — seven likes on posts, photos and
comments, and two comments — before the first exchange. (The export records
only Dan's own actions, so this asymmetry is a property of the source, not
proof she never reciprocated.)

The first documented conversation is a single day: **July 5, 2013**, 21
messages in Facebook Messenger, an afternoon burst and a late-night one. It
already contains the whole later relationship in miniature. He opens with a
disaster story told as a bid (*"I got beat up by 2 ex gf's
yesterday...simultaneously. Thought you might understand. What is life but
shit?"*), names the frame outright three messages later (*"That's probably
not what I should be telling my celeb crush alu"*), and closes the night
asking her to *"sign my alu fanfic essay book."* Two days earlier he had
told Polyak, of her, *"She's so dreamy"* — and Polyak had answered *"The
only thing I like more than my new stoner girlfriend is the immaculate ally
lubin truth."* She, for her part, is dry and unimpressed and keeps him at
exactly one arm's length: *"Weren't you dating some girl forever and living
in BK?"*

Then five and a half years of nothing.

## August–October 2019: the crisis market

Seventy-four percent of the lifetime volume of this relationship falls inside
seventy-seven days, and the trigger is her losing her job.

The phone-handle thread opens on June 28, 2019 with one line from her, then
goes quiet until **August 7, 2019, 22:39** — *"Hi / Not good lol / Lost my
job / Spiraling / Miss u."* From there to October 22 the thread runs 942
messages. What happens inside it is the conversion of a fifteen-year crush
into a market.

The mechanism is explicit and it is Dan's. Within ninety minutes of learning
she has no income he has offered to fund a webcam career, sent an OnlyFans
referral link, promised a ring light, and said *"i'll help financially."*
Ten days later, on August 17, the framing has become a price list: *"lol
help me here - how do i try to sell you on letting me pay for your noodz
while you're in bummer island? i can solve these financial problems! also
the self esteem/self image ones too."* She counters (*"More for a video but
$50 for a tit pic"*), he counters back (*"i'd do $30 for a tit pic if your
face is visible… that is like SUPER PREMIUM princess pricing"*), and the
transaction closes over Cash App the same night. On September 25 he
advertises: *"public service announcement: i just got a nice check in, so if
you are drunk or just want money...i'll GLADLY overpay for a peek."* On
September 7 he proposes the arrangement by name — *"let's work out some kind
of sugar baby/daddy situation"* — and on September 1 he offers to fly her to
New York with *"promise 1: $500 on arrival"* and the unprompted rider *"and
nooooo you're not obligated to fuck me."*

The audit of the whole arrangement is his, delivered on **October 22, 2019**
in the middle of an argument about it:

> **DAN:** lady i've sent 2100 and have like 2 pictures and im STILL trying lol
> **ALLY:** You have not sent me $2100
> **DAN:** *[screenshot]*
> **ALLY:** Oh fuck

Three hours later, closing the same night: *"what a perfectly appropriate
ending of a convo to sum up our history. me sending you money and confessing
i think you're the most perfect and literally my dream girl and you don't
even have the decency to allow me to see you naked **so i can get the poison
out**."*

That last clause is the load-bearing sentence on this page. The goal is not
possession and never was; it is discharge. He says the same thing from the
other direction three weeks earlier — asked whether they would get along in
person, he answers *"You'd be scared of me irl"* → *"that is so fucking
understated you have no idea… i'm always super nervy around girls for a

then, October 22: *"i have NEVER felt so hypnotized by someone i've never
seen or met in my life. whatever you are, is what perfect is...to me at
least."* The inaccessibility is not an obstacle to the attachment. It is the
attachment's operating condition, which is why seventeen years of it have
cost him money and produced no meeting.

The other half of the ledger is hers, and it should not be softened. Across
the same three months she reports: no income since July, no health
insurance, a bottle of wine a night, a month off weed, Wellbutrin making her
*"more suicidal,"* seven Xanax being *"saved for a rainy day,"* *"If I
killed myself would you be sad,"* *"I told my mom the other day if I killed
myself it would be her fault,"* and *"What if I don't make it until 30?"*
Dan's responses run from genuine (*"you're gonna be fine"*; *"i really think
a lot of what you're feeling is more of a shared societal experience than
you think"*) to instrumental in the same hour (*"you're really not allowed
to even joke about that because of my renewed interest in you and your
luscious titzzzz"*). He is buying photographs from a woman who is telling
him, in the same thread, that she is planning her own funeral. Both facts
are on the record and the page states both.

She also names the arrangement she is in, once, in her own idiom: *"You
don't want me on your payroll / I want to be on your payroll tho / But you
scare me."*

## What he told her that he told no one else

let you know i'm a garbage person so you don't have to worry about feeling

## The reversal (2020–2025)

After October 2019 the volume collapses and never recovers, and the
direction of neglect inverts.

June 8, 2020 is the last substantial conversation on the phone handle: 51
messages, pandemic New York, her thirtieth birthday eighteen days out
(*"My whole life I've joked about being 30 and now I'm actually here"*), and
Dan's proposal, unchanged — *"if i buy you a long distance vibe, would you
let me pay to control it… i just got my unemployment back pay."*

Then thirty-eight months of silence, broken on August 30, 2023 by her: *"Send
me money I'm sad and need a little treat."*

The October 30, 2023 exchange is the relationship's other hinge. Dan opens
to tell her he had put his cat down that day; her second message is *"So do
you only reach out when you need something?"*, and then *"Idk I feel like
I've tried to keep in touch and you've been kind of a dick."* He does not
deny it — he pleads general defect (*"Also, I can refer you to my mother and
everyone else I know to confirm that my communication is very
inconsistent"*; *"I'm a bad person / And I am bad at texting back / Until
I'm not"*) and then reframes it as impersonal: *"you shouldn't feel like
it's anything specific to you that is the cause of my bad response record."*
Her answer is the sharpest sentence anyone directs at him in this thread:
*"Well I do / It's personal lol."*

The man who spent ten years watching her and a year begging for a photograph
is, five years on, the one not answering. That inversion — and its timing,
inside the 2021–2023 employment block when the whole corpus goes quiet — is
the reason this page is filed as an instance of
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/dormancy-not-exit|dormancy, not exit]] rather than of
pursuit.

Contact thereafter is thin and one-directional in her favour: November 14,
2023, *"Are you still rich I have a huge favor"* (no reply on record);
March 16–18, 2025, *"Let me get $100"*; and a single *"Lololol"* with an
attachment on November 17, 2025, which is the last message she sends in the
corpus.

This is the finding the page is organised around, and it makes the whole

crush attached; she is the **designated overflow channel** — the place the
attachment goes when the primary bond is unavailable, and the reason the
channel has to stay unconsummated is that a consummated one could fail. Look
at what each activation is adjacent to: December 2018 opens inside the deep
cycle and the Annie/Alexis reunion arrangement; August–October 2019 runs
concurrently with Annie's escorting, the acid meltdown, the Bryan encounter
and the funeral collapse; June 2020 is the pandemic; October 2023 is the cat
dying; March 2025 is the separation period; June 1, 2026 is nineteen hours
after the end. The channel has no idle traffic. Every burst is a failure
somewhere else.

**Prediction.** If this reading is right, the next contact with Ally will
follow a rupture in Dan's primary attachment within days, not precede one,
and it will originate from Dan and go unanswered. **What would falsify it:**
a sustained Ally-initiated exchange during a period of relational stability,
or any documented meeting in person — which, in seventeen years and 1,300
messages, has never once been scheduled by either of them.
