# Annie Ulmer — 2026-08-26 Boundary Update

## The surprising thing is how uninteresting the boundary is becoming

The August 16–19 rupture was spectacular by the standards of this relationship. The important August 26 observation is almost the opposite: **Dan reports that Annie has remained blocked for several days, and the block is not currently being used as a dramatic event.**

That matters because the historical pattern made severance itself part of the intensity cycle. A declaration of no contact could be followed by recontact, crisis, procurement, reconciliation, or another round of escalation. The August 16–19 record even contains a declared block that was not executed.

The current report is different in one narrow but important respect: Dan says he has kept the block in place and has not been dwelling on Annie, scheming about Annie, or trying to find a roundabout way back into the relationship.

That claim should be tested over time rather than believed because it is eloquent.

## The new evidence is behavioral

Dan tells Ally that he has had Annie blocked for several days and describes the achievement not as punishment but as a hill he needed to get over. He acknowledges that he required support and inspiration to do it and admits that he tends to solve things through roundabout means.

Ally's response is notably unsentimental. She does not congratulate him for defeating Annie or tell him that the relationship was meaningless. Instead, she points out the more general problem: he is giving another person too much power over his life.

Dan agrees that this was true.

The exchange then moves away from Annie.

That last part is easy to overlook and may be the most significant part of the record.

## The Ally channel is not functioning as a replacement crisis

On August 26 Dan is talking to Ally, flirting with her, joking with her, watching documentaries she recommends, and following her advice about his father. But he is not using her to manufacture a new version of the Annie relationship.

Ally herself calls out the possibility that he is looking to fill a void. Dan does not answer by making Ally responsible for filling it. Instead he accepts the criticism and continues the conversation at lower intensity.

That gives the current configuration a useful structural description:

**Annie = closed boundary.**

**Ally = open relationship with explicit skepticism and ordinary contact.**

**Dan = attempting to tolerate the difference without forcing either channel to become the other.**

If that remains true, it is a meaningful departure from the older architecture.

## What would falsify this update

This page should be considered failed if any of the following occurs:

- Annie is unblocked and the old contact cycle resumes;
- Dan begins using Ally primarily to regulate distress about Annie;
- Ally is pressured to certify that Dan has changed;
- the block becomes a theatrical threat rather than an actual boundary;
- the current low-intensity contact is followed by a new spike of romantic or crisis-driven behavior;
- Dan treats a few days of success as proof that the work is finished.

The last one is especially important.

The record has plenty of turning points. What it has very little of is **boring continuity after the turning point**.

## Why this may actually matter

If the block holds, if the Ally channel remains ordinary, and if Dan continues accepting correction without needing to convert it into a grand emotional event, then the August rupture may have produced something the previous severances did not: **a stable behavioral boundary that does not require emotional warfare to maintain.**

That would not mean the relationship with Annie has been emotionally erased. It would mean something more useful: the relationship no longer automatically dictates Dan's behavior.

That is the claim this page should test.
