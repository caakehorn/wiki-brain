# Eric Jester

Eric Jester is a Full Sail friend (contacts notes: "sandwich eater. dream
killer.") whose relationship with Dan is documented across two
completely different, previously unmerged sources — a Facebook Messenger
thread running January 2009 to September 2020, and an iMessage/SMS
thread running May 2017 to April 2025 — that together push the
documented friendship back to **January 2009**, seven years earlier and
predating the "2017-05-08" start this page previously carried by nearly
a decade. The relationship is sparse but genuinely warm: check-ins that
resurface every year or two, each one picking back up exactly where the
last left off, with shared friends [[wiki/people/jason-bermejo|Jason
Bermejo]], [[wiki/people/matt-dunn|Matt Dunn]], and
[[wiki/people/jamie-mohler|Jamie Mohler]] from the same Orlando/Full
Sail cohort. Buried inside the Facebook side of the thread — a source
the previous version of this page never opened — is the corpus's single
most detailed first-person account of the end of Fran Coldren's life,
told by Dan to Eric within a day of it happening.

## The Facebook years (2009–2020)

The record opens January 28, 2009 with Eric thanking Dan, deadpan, "for
letting me come by and smoke all your weed last night" — consistent
with a shared Full Sail class that spring (a February 2009 exchange has
Eric formally apologizing for a joke about Dan's grandmother's death,
made "in class," that Dan had taken hard: "My grandmother was one of
the sweetest people I have ever met... I don't tell everyone about the
fact that I found her after she had a stroke the day of my birthday
party"). By September 2011 the two are trading real news rather than
just jokes: Dan, then in New York, is actively participating in Occupy
Wall Street — "was there all day yesterday... dropping supplies off
after work and trying to catch the general assembly at night... the
population in zuccotti is so different now than it was last week too...
lots of suburbia is showing up" — a level of on-the-ground engagement
not otherwise documented this early in the corpus's political record,
years before the "2020 left turn" the wiki dates elsewhere. The
following month, October 2, 2011, Eric tells his own story from the
other side of a bar: suspended from his bartending job after nearly
coming to blows with a drunk, condescending customer, the actual
flashpoint being a thrown lemon rather than a punch ("i eventually told
him 'fuck this' and threw a lemon at him... they didn't really want to
suspend me... but they had to do something").

The thread runs through the usual register of college-friend Facebook
correspondence for the next several years — inside jokes ("Ethan Kray"),
shared music (Jonny Craig/Dance Gavin Dance gossip), and check-ins after
Eric moves back to Orlando in April 2013. Immediately after that move,
on **April 15–16, 2013**, Eric checks in about the Boston Marathon
bombing — "Thinking of you with this shit going on. I know the
Jester-house is far from the carnage" — and Dan's reply supplies a
previously undocumented near-miss: "Kendel was 2 blocks away from it
when it happened. She's fine but I'd been freaking out all day." This is
new, personal-stakes texture for the same event a separate capture
already credits with "days of no sleep watching the April 2013 Boston
Marathon manhunt unfold" as the first attested instance of Dan's
macro-scale chaos preference
([[wiki/timeline/events/franki-fireworks-day-2013]]) — the manhunt
wasn't only compelling to watch, someone in his own life was nearly
caught in the blast itself. Three further dated exchanges from this
stretch carry real weight beyond the jokes:

- **May 9, 2013** — Dan resurfaces an old video and tells Eric "that's
  12 year old dan telling everyone about how 'rollerblading teaches me
  about who i am.'" Eric's reply — "Want to know what's more
  embarrassing? I've already seen that." — is independent, external
  confirmation that Dan's childhood rollerblading identity (age ~11–17,
  early-to-mid 2000s — no dedicated wiki page exists for this era as of
  this pass) left a durable enough public trace, a video evidently
  already in circulation, to become a running joke a decade later
  between college friends who never knew Dan as a child.
- **July 5, 2013, 1:31 AM** — Dan: "dude i got punched in the face / by
  alexis today hahaha / i have a lot of things to tell you that you're
  gonna be real proud of me for." [[wiki/timeline/events/franki-fireworks-day-2013]]
  documents Alexis discovering the Franki affair by text "roughly ten
  minutes into" a car ride "in early July 2013," with an explicit,
  standing note that the exact date "is not fixed." This message is the
  first primary-dated evidence located for that day anywhere in the
  corpus — it narrows "early July" to the July 4–5 weekend specifically,
  and it adds a fact absent from every other account: whatever happened
  when Alexis found out escalated to a physical blow, not just a text.

The thread goes quiet after a February 2014 "remember '09" callback,
resurfaces briefly in 2016 (a photo exchange, "Mawfuckin J. Haven't hung
out with him in a few years" — the "J" unidentified, plausibly but not
confirmably Jason Bermejo), and closes on Facebook with a garbled,
apparently intoxicated exchange on September 23, 2020 — misspelled
fragments, a half-formed political jab about "history" and being "a non
resiste[nt] nazi," and a run of empty messages — that Eric mostly
declines to engage with ("Oh gawd"). No further Facebook activity is
preserved after that day.

## The vigil, in Dan's own words (April 5–6, 2018)

The single most significant find in this thread is not about Eric at
all — it is Dan's own account of the end of
[[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]]'s life, volunteered to Eric within
weeks of her death and preserved nowhere else in this much detail. Eric
opens on April 5, 2018 with a condolence — "I saw on Facebook about your
great grandmother. I'm sorry to hear it man. She was an awesome lady" —
and Dan, rather than simply accepting it, tells the whole story:

He had been paid **$15 an hour** to look after her full-time for the
preceding six months ("$15 an hour to play video games with gram is a
good deal"), a caregiving arrangement independently documented on
[[wiki/timeline/events/fran-death-vigil]]. When hospice began and
feeding stopped, he sat with her for four days waiting for the end.
On the last night, in the room with his girlfriend, he silently tested
something he says he doesn't believe in: *"i said to myself 'if you can
hear me give me some kind of sign.' my girlfriend who didn't know i was
doing this shakes me awake immediately after and tells me she blinked
for the first time in a week. i went over to her bed and something
happened and she was like there again... you know how little credit i
give supernatural or religious ideas. i'm the last person who would be
pushing a bullshit story about something spiritual happening."* He adds,
almost as an aside, that he has the whole thing on video.

This is a second, independent telling of the same core act — a silent
mental "test" at Fran's bedside, validated by a startled girlfriend —
that [[wiki/timeline/events/fran-death-vigil]] already documents from a
same-night Facebook message to Lucie Dobbin, and the two accounts do not
match in every detail. Told to Lucie the night it happened: Dan stays
awake, silently asks if Fran can hear him, **she wakes and smiles**
almost immediately, and dies within minutes. Told to Eric a day or two
later: Dan is **falling asleep**, his girlfriend wakes **him**
immediately after **Fran blinks** for the first time in a week, and Fran
"was like there again... she looked the same even" — recovering
presence, not the death itself. Both center on the same act and the
same corroborating witness (the girlfriend), but disagree on who fell
asleep, what the sign was, and whether it preceded death by minutes or
happened during an earlier, separate night of the four-day vigil. Both
tellings are Dan's own, within 48 hours of each other, to two different
friends — recorded here rather than harmonized into one version. The next day
he sums it up in three lines that carry the entire arrangement's
emotional logic in miniature: *"she was a trip dude but i spent the past
six months looking after her full time... and we had a lot of fun. got
good closure and finally did something in my life that wasn't
completely selfish haha... and she got a poetic ended. what else can
you ask for."* In the same breath he names what comes next: *"now that
i'm not saddled here with gram anymore... and that inheritance money
coming... ya boy is getting out of PA"* — dating the intention to leave
Pennsylvania on inheritance money to literally the week after her death,
not a later, more considered decision.

Eric reciprocates with his own grandfather's death, and the two spend
the rest of the exchange comparing notes on family, money, and
industry — Eric's student-loan math on Full Sail ("until 2025... but
that gets extended... I'm losing my fucking hearing" from bartending),
his own long relationship, and an extended, entirely characteristic
digression into the couple's non-monogamy that ends with Eric concluding
"That's the most Dan Frank thing I've ever heard."

This is new to the wiki in this level of granularity. `fran-death-vigil.md`
already carries the $15/hour figure from other sources; the bedside
"sign" moment, the video, and the "poetic ended" framing did not appear
anywhere in the corpus before this pass.

## The iMessage years (2017–2025)

Once the SMS/iMessage record opens (May 8, 2017 — "Do you still have
the final bounce of our studio project"), the register stays consistent
with the Facebook years: sparse, warm, resuming without friction. Eric
works in film/TV as bartender-adjacent crew, living in Texas with a
longtime girlfriend (later wife) he describes as unusually unjealous
about an industry where "sex is just thrown at you day after day" — a
dynamic he frames candidly and without judgment, matching the tone of
the April 2018 exchange above. His April 8, 2019 engagement announcement
(five years together, "because we actually like each other") also
carries an unrelated but independently valuable data point: Dan tells
him he'd "paid $1500 last week to have pornstar trinity st clair come
hang out with my girlfriend and i for exactly one hour" — a specific
figure not preserved in
[[wiki/people/trinity-st-clair|Trinity St. Clair]]'s own thread, dating
that encounter to roughly the last week of March 2019. Eric loses a
beloved cat, Gabe, on October 30, 2023 ("the cat that made me love
cats!") — the same date and framing that independently resolves the
ambiguous "Gabe" reference on [[wiki/people/jason-bermejo]] as a pet,
not a person.

A recurring bit runs through 2025: [[wiki/people/matt-dunn|Matt Dunn]]
Venmo-requesting Eric $5 every year on his birthday, without fail, which
Eric always pays ("Every damn year. And it's always $5. Of course I pay
it," April 22, 2025) — the cohort's longest-running documented joke,
independently confirmed a year later from Jason's side of the friend
group. Eric also supplies the only pre-2025 dated sighting of Dunn as an
active presence rather than a punchline: on November 16, 2018 he reports
"Dunn is down in that neighborhood right now… He was snapping me videos
of full sail" — Dunn back at the Orlando campus itself, sending
unprompted nostalgia footage seven years after the cohort's own
attendance window.

## Gaps

plausibly Jason Bermejo given the shared cohort, but nothing in either
thread ties the initial to a full name. The image attachment referenced
in the January 2011 Facebook exchange was not retrieved. Whether Eric
