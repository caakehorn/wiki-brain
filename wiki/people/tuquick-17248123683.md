## The name (August 8, 2026)

On August 8, 2026 Dan ran the handle through **FOREWARN**, a commercial
background-check product, and recorded the result as a capture. The name it
returned is **Jerel Wayne Coles**, 36, of Uniontown, Pennsylvania.

The identification is not a guess, and the reason is that the corpus supplied
half of it. FOREWARN lists two numbers for Coles, and the mobile —
`(724) 812-3683`, last seen November 2022 — is **the same number this page has
been built on since June 2026**. The wiki had the handle first and the name
second; they meet on a digit-for-digit match rather than on a name search.
That is as strong as an identification of this kind gets without a document
signed by the person.

Two things follow, and they are worth separating.

**Uniontown.** Coles's address history places him in Uniontown from September
2024, with a prior Connellsville address running from 2008. Both are Fayette
County. This matters for one open question and not for the reason it might
appear to: [[wiki/people/the-unnamed-man]] — the July 2026 third party Dan has
deliberately refused to identify — is placed in Uniontown by Annie's own
account, and the corpus has never been able to say whether the two men are one
man. The identification **does not close that question**. What it does is
remove a way of closing it in the negative: "Tuquick lives somewhere else" is
no longer available as a distinguishing fact. Everything else about the
question is exactly as open as it was.

**Category of source.** FOREWARN is a data aggregator sold to real-estate
agents, not a court system, and it is the first source of its kind in this
corpus. The tiering in `EXTRACTION_SPEC.md` does not cover it, so state it
plainly: the *phone-to-name* link is a direct record match and is strong; the
*name-to-criminal-record* links are matched probabilistically, and the capture
preserves FOREWARN's own admission of this — the sample record lists its
"Match Key Criteria Met" as first name, last name, middle initial, birth date,
city, state and proximity. Nothing below has been verified against a Fayette
County docket, and none of it should be quoted as though it had been.
