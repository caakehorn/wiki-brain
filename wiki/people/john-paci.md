# John Paci

John Paci was the landlord and owner of [[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st|307
E 76th St]] for the entire six-year tenancy Dan and
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] held there (February 2019 – February
2025) and personally managed the building himself — leasing, exterminator
scheduling, rent and debt collection all ran through him directly rather
than a management company. The operator's own characterization, offered
unprompted in a 2026-08-10 capture — "elderly i-talian owner of the
building and probably NYC's sweetest and nicest landlord" — is consistent
with the corpus's own record of him: Annie's first description of him,
September 2020, calls him "a GREAT guy," and Dan's own gloss the same day
— "he's the one who stuck his neck out for us and held the apartment when
we found it" — describes someone who took a chance on them rather than a
purely transactional owner.

That warmth did not prevent him from being owed a substantial amount of
money when the tenancy ended. By 2023 the couple had accumulated debt
against him large enough to require scheduled payments ($650 sent in
November 2024, "to tell John"). On March 5, 2025, weeks after Dan's
departure, Paci wrote to close the account: "Dan, after paying to have
the remainder of the stuff you left removed, and deducting the security
deposit. I rounded the balance down to an even $10,000.00. Let me know
when you can begin to pay it down. John." He remained in contact with
Annie afterward — a building-wide group text under his name recurs
(collecting October rent for the building in 2023, scheduling an
exterminator for the building in December 2024), and Annie's own annoyed
reaction to a Paci text in July 2025 ("Fucking John Paci just texted
me") suggests the debt, or something adjacent to it, was still an active
subject four months after Dan left New York.

## Gaps

Whether any part of the final $10,000 balance was ever paid down is
unknown — no correspondence past July 2025 was located. Paci's full name,
age, and any other properties he owns or manages are undocumented; the
"Jr" in one contact-card entry ("John Paci Jr") is unexplained and may
indicate a family business rather than sole ownership.
