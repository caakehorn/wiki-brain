# Taste Profile from Favorites

The 2026-06-23 page treated 2,016 curated entries as a consumer identity:
an eclectic but focused listener, a politically intense reader, an
architect-tagged visual collector, aligned with a psychometric spine. That
reading was the same error [[wiki/interests/favorites/eclecticism]] has
since retired, filed one directory over, on the same date, from the same
CSV. **Dan does not have a taste profile in the consumer sense.** He has
a small number of live functions whose residue was filed as favorites.
Once the dual-architecture reading is applied — observational domains
close bounded sets through unique witnesses; the production domain keeps
functional clusters in parallel because they are being *used* — the
entire list collapses to six jobs: three music functions maintained since
adolescence, two events triangulated on paper, and one visual subject
collected through twenty-five hands. The counts survive. The sentence
"eclectic but focused consumer" does not.
