# 307 E 76th St (Manhattan)

307 E 76th St, Upper East Side, was home for the entire second New York
chapter — February 2019 to February 2025 — shared with
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]], and the stage for the relationship's
last whole years and its rupture. The six years at this address contain the
2019 hard-left politicization (Bernie + Chapo), the pandemic bunker years,
the 2020–21 market era ([[wiki/timeline/periods/2020-2021-market-era]]),
the 41-month Au Za'atar job a downtown train ride away
([[wiki/work/au-zaatar]]), the 2024 political-obsession peak, and — in its
final autumn — the concealed [[wiki/people/eli|Eli]] affair whose January 2025 discovery ended
the household ([[wiki/timeline/events/eli-incident]]): Annie was found
unconscious with the coworker in a building lobby ~80 minutes after her
shift, location sharing off. Dan left the address, and New York, on
February 22, 2025 — under an eviction he had asked the landlord to
pretend to file.

## How the tenancy ended

The apartment did not evict the household; the household evicted itself
through the apartment. Between roughly January 10 and February 1, 2025,
Dan called Paci, told him the family had to vacate because his father had
suffered a TBI and he would be travelling back constantly — the TBI true,
the constant travel invented — and asked him, as a favour, to say he was
filing an eviction if Annie telephoned to check [OPERATOR]. Paci agreed.
Dan then told Annie the eviction was Paci's own decision, provoked by her
having vomited in the building hallway on the night Eli brought her home.

The address is where that performance is visible, because the household's
two occupants were getting different accounts of the same building. On
2025-01-11 15:35 ET Dan wrote to Annie "I am calling John back now. I'm
not asking for extra time. I need to know TODAY what you want - you want
to be with me or you want to stay in New York" — the landlord call and
the relationship ultimatum in a single message [RAW-DUMP]. Twelve minutes
later Annie replied "I'll be contacting John as well bc I was not aware
of this having to be out by feb 1," and three minutes after *that* Dan
answered "I was on the phone with him as you sent that" and "And told him
you would also be calling him to confirm" [RAW-DUMP]. That last line is
the strongest artifact the address produces: Dan telling Annie, in
writing, that he had pre-briefed the landlord for her verification call,
in a register where it reads as diligence.

The pattern holds for the rest of the tenancy's last month. Every piece of
information Annie received about her own home arrived through Dan. On
2025-02-05 she asked what Paci had said four separate times — "? John
said we had to be out"; "Can you please tell me what John said and your
mom"; "What did John say."; "What did John say dab." — and received "Talked
to John" and "My mom is going to come get us later next week. John said
okay" [RAW-DUMP]. Her one independent channel to the building, the
telephone, had already been closed by the January 11 pre-brief.

The performance succeeded and the plan failed anyway. Annie refused to
stay in Pennsylvania, and Dan said so in writing three days before the
move, to a New York contact: "We are most likely going to be back here to
stay again before too long. Annie doesn't want to leave" (2025-02-19
20:23 ET) [RAW-DUMP]. Full account of the arrangement, its seven steps and
its four independent corroborations: [[wiki/people/john-paci]]. The
period the exit opened: [[wiki/timeline/periods/feb-apr-2025-return-and-rupture]].

## The cast

**[[wiki/people/john-paci|John Paci]]** owned the building and ran it
personally for the entire tenancy — leasing it directly ("he's the one who
stuck his neck out for us and held the apartment when we found it," per
Annie, September 2020), scheduling exterminators himself by text, and
collecting rent and debt payments by name into at least mid-2025, five
months after Dan had left. He is the addressee and signatory on the
building's final word on the tenancy: a March 5, 2025 message rounding
the couple's outstanding balance to an even $10,000 and asking Dan to
begin paying it down (see "The debt," below). He is also, on the 2026-08-13
decode, the one person outside the household who knew the eviction was
theatre — which reframes rather than contradicts the warmth the corpus
records of him: agreeing to play the villain for a tenant already five
figures in arrears is a larger favour than holding an apartment. Full
account at [[wiki/people/john-paci]].

**[[wiki/people/jim-blanchard|Jim Blanchard]]** is named by the operator
as "world's worst building super/real estate agent" — a second figure,
distinct from Paci, who the capture implies handled the property's
day-to-day management or leasing. The corpus corroborates only that a
real James Blanchard existed and was known to Dan (a single November 15,
2019 message: "i'm in jim blanchards apartment," sent to a Uniontown-area
contact, plus a Facebook contact card tagging him "Nyc"). Nothing in the
recovered record ties Blanchard specifically to 307 E 76th St, the
super role, or a negative characterization — the sole primary trace
predates the operator's framing of him and doesn't confirm it. Treated
here as operator testimony about a real person whose specific role at
this address remains unconfirmed; see [[wiki/people/jim-blanchard]] and
its own Gaps. A separate 57-row thread on `+19173631636` that the
2026-08-10 pass mistakenly read as Paci's own building-wide group text
turns out to belong to a building neighbour, "Jim," who refers to Paci in
the third person throughout — that correction is worked in full on
[[wiki/people/john-paci]] and substantially corroborates a Jim at this
address, though not yet the surname.

## The debt

The debt is the one part of the tenancy that was real from beginning to
end, and it is also the part whose final increment was deliberate.

By 2023, Dan and Annie had accumulated a balance to Paci substantial
enough that they were making scheduled payments against it — $650 sent in
November 2024, for instance, explicitly earmarked "to tell John." The
operator's own account of the arrangement is that the balance behaved
exactly the way the payments suggest: "we owed a bunch of money, were
paying weekly rent for a long time with additional money on it each week
to chip away at the balance, and yet our balance continued to grow when we
would fuck something up" [OPERATOR]. That is the passive accumulation this
page previously described, and for roughly five years it is the whole
story.

It stops being the whole story in January 2025. Step four of the seven-step
plan was to "spend 2 months building up any money that came in by not
paying any new rent and adding that to the balance, knowing that i wasn't
going to pay any of it anyways" [OPERATOR] — the withheld rent converted
into moving capital, with the growth of the balance as an accepted cost
rather than an unwanted result. The message record is consistent with
that and dates its start precisely: Annie's last statement of having paid
rent is 2025-01-05 15:40 ET ("that leaves me with barely any money left
for the week since I paid all of the rent"), and a sweep of every record
between 2025-01-01 and the February 22 move-out returns **no further
rent payment to Paci in either direction** [DERIVED — scope: all 217,573
records of `raw/self/dox-scan/all_imessages_complete_dump.txt`,
America/New_York, both directions, all handles; absence in one file is not
proof of non-payment, but it is the only file with trustworthy direction].

The debt was never retired. On March 5, 2025 — eleven days after Dan's
departure — Paci wrote: "Dan, after paying to have the remainder of the
stuff you left removed, and deducting the security deposit. I rounded the
balance down to an even $10,000.00. Let me know when you can begin to pay
it down. John." Four months later he was still asking ("Any chance of
collecting some back rent?", 2025-07-25 11:18 ET), and Annie's own
reaction to a Paci text seven minutes later ("Fucking John Paci just
texted me") is consistent with the debt remaining an active, unpleasant
subject well after the household dissolved. Neither July 25 message has a
reply in the record. Whether any of the $10,000 was ever paid is not
recovered.

The dyadic Paci thread that carries all of this is small: **26 records,
13 Sent and 13 Received, spanning 2024-03-04 to 2025-07-25** [DERIVED —
scope: `+16314558185` only, both directions,
`raw/self/dox-scan/all_imessages_complete_dump.txt`, America/New_York].
Six years of tenancy produced twenty-six surviving texts with the
landlord, and none at all across the 166 days between 2024-08-18 10:22
and 2025-02-01 07:26 — the window in which the arrangement was made
(elapsed time, matching [[wiki/people/john-paci]]; the capture's "167"
counts calendar dates).

## Which building, which apartment

The 2026-08-10 capture that produced most of the cast carries a `target:`
field reading **"306 E. 76th Street, Apt 6C"** while its own title reads
"# 307 E. 76th Street." Neither half of the target field survives the
corpus, and the discrepancy is worth stating rather than silently
normalising, because a wrong street number would break every
location-history figure on this page.

## The roach bombing and the September 2020 trip to PA

On September 10, 2020, Dan told Annie the apartment was scheduled for
extermination: "sorry to keep bugging you just wanted to give you an
update. they are bombing the apt tomorrow around 11 or so." The couple
relocated to Annie's parents' home in Uniontown for what became roughly a
week — Dan references "the past week, especially the first 4 days of the
trip" in a message sent nine days later. That nine-to-ten-day stay is the
same trip during which the relationship's [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Train
Plan crisis]] happened: on the night of September 19, mid-argument, Dan
is simultaneously coordinating a **second** exterminator visit directly
with Paci — "i'm on the line with john paci tomorrow… nobody is to enter
that apartment, other than me and the exterminators" — while the
relationship blows up around him. By the next morning both Dan and Annie
are texting about packing, animals, and driving back, and Dan separately
tells a PA-area contact "it's dan, suzanne's son" the following day,
suggesting the household relocated in full, dog and cats included ("i got
all my stuff packed and the dog and cats"). The roach problem was not a
one-time event: a second, unrelated infestation shows up in the corpus
in December 2020 and February 2021 ("I just found a fucking roach!!!!
I'm furious"), and exterminator visits recur as late as December 2024
("Exterminator will be at 307 on Monday morning. John PACI").

The operator's own memory of the sequence — that the bombing happened
"right after the train incident" — has the order reversed from what the
corpus shows: the bombing (Sept 10–11) precedes the Train Plan discovery
(Sept 19–20) by about nine days, and the two exterminator visits (the
original bombing and the one Dan is arranging on the night of the
blowup) are easy to conflate in memory into one event. Both readings
converge on the same underlying fact, which is the one worth keeping:
**the trip to PA that hosted the Train Plan crisis had a mundane,
previously undocumented cause.** Nobody had connected the two before this
pass.

That inversion is worth holding alongside the 2026-08-13 decode, because
the two captures fail in opposite directions and the difference is
instructive. On the roaches, the operator's *sequence* was wrong and the
corpus corrected it. On the eviction, the corpus reads perfectly on its
own — a landlord's threat, a tenant's apology, a balance — and only the
operator can correct it, because the record was curated at the time to
survive exactly this kind of reading. A capture is testimony either way;
what varies is whether the corpus is in a position to check it.

## Gaps

Jim Blanchard's actual role at this address is unconfirmed beyond the
operator's own account — a targeted search by his email
(`jtblanchard66@gmail.com`) or phone across the fuller export set (not
just the primary dump) might surface more, and the newly-identified
neighbour "Jim" on `+19173631636` may or may not be the same man. Whether
Dan or Annie ever paid any part of the final $10,000 balance is unknown;
no further Paci correspondence past July 2025 was located. **Nothing in
Paci's own voice confirms the arrangement** — his half of it lives
entirely in telephone calls — so the address's terminal event rests on
one operator capture plus circumstantial structure in the record. The
lease's original signatory, the monthly rent, the security deposit amount,
and how the roach infestation started were not recovered; the "Apt 6C" in
the 2026-08-10 capture's target field has no corpus trace and no
explanation [UNRESOLVED]. What the withheld two months of rent actually
amounted to in cash is not derivable either: without the monthly rent
figure, the size of the moving capital the plan raised cannot be
computed, and it is not stated anywhere in `raw/`.
