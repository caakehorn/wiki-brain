# The Franki Fireworks Day (early July 2013)

On an afternoon in early July 2013, Dan took one of his mother's cars, drove to
15 Holly Lane in Uniontown, and picked up [[wiki/people/franki-faris|Franki
Faris]] — a girl he knew from junior high and high school — with no destination
in mind. Within roughly ten minutes, a text arrived from
[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]], his partner of six years: **"I saw your
messages. I know you're with Franki."** Rather than turn around, he withdrew
$350 from his mother's ATM card, bought a quarter ounce of cocaine from the
family's supplier, and stayed out for more than ten hours. He describes the day
in his own words as **"one of the more pivotal days of my young adult life,"**
and the record supports the assessment: the six-year relationship ended, a
pattern he would name and re-run in 2015 was established, and the music
production identity began in the same season.

The day is documented from a single operator capture written in 2026 —
thirteen years after the fact — so it is memory, not residue. It is marked
`knowledge: mixed` accordingly, and the parts of it that other sources can
check are flagged below.

### The fireworks date the day, almost

The page for Franki has carried a standing note since 2026-07-13 that the "July
4" framing used elsewhere in the corpus **had no source anywhere in the
archive** and was flagged rather than adopted. This capture is the first source
for it. It does not name the date — but a municipal fireworks show at the mall,
visible from the Rt 119 bypass, in early July, is a July-4-adjacent event in
practical terms. The framing is now **supported but still not explicitly dated**,
which is a real improvement on unsourced and short of settled.

## What the day detonated

**The confrontation is now verbatim.** [[wiki/people/franki-faris]] recorded
that the wording and date of Alexis's challenge *"hasn't been located verbatim,
only referenced retrospectively."* It has been located: *"I saw your messages. I
know you're with Franki."* The mechanism is also newly specific — she was
reading his messages, prompted by something in his behaviour, which makes the
discovery an act of detection rather than a coincidence.

**Alexis was displaced to a property the wiki did not know was in play.** She
moved to *"suz's other place at 155 virginia avenue,"* stayed five days, and then
returned to her parents in Carmichaels. [[wiki/places/155-virginia-ave]] dates
its own story from a **January 2015** lease. This puts the address under
[[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]]'s control **eighteen months earlier**, and
already performing the function the later page calls lair-continuity: housing a
partner who has been displaced from the primary residence. The lair predates the
tenancy.

**Suz then engineered the aftermath.** Per the capture, she thought it a good
idea to have Alexis and [[wiki/people/danielle-onesi|Danielle Onesi]] — Dan's
first girlfriend, and Alexis's *"former enemy"* — spend time together and bond
over their shared grievance, *"and this caused a huge thing."* That is the
maternal switchboard operating on the relational graph rather than the financial
one, and it is the earliest documented instance of it.

## The other two things that started that summer

**The production identity has a date now.** The capture records that the DJ
controller Dan had acquired while working at ishlab and *"never really touched"*
was picked up after the return from New York, and that *"by summer, I was
dedicating my time to that — it is right as this began to take hold that I
remember this all taking place."* [[wiki/interests/music/overview]] has carried
the origin as *"operating since approximately 2013."* This is the first source
that dates it inside the year and attaches it to a cause: an idle depressive
interval after a failed New York attempt, with a piece of equipment already in
the room.

**The Boston manhunt is the chaos preference's missing exhibit.** Describing the
months between the return and this day, Dan records almost nothing except *"a
few days of no sleep as i watched the boston bombing and subsequent manhunt
unfold"* — and, separately, a disappointing binge of *Arrested Development*.
[[wiki/mind/concepts/chaos-preference]] names as its most serious gap that *"the
Boston-bombing coverage response used to frame the request is not attested in
the searched corpus."* It is attested now. The detail that carries it is not
that he watched, but that he did not sleep for days doing so, during a period he
otherwise describes as apathetic depression. The entropy appetite was the one
thing running.

## The taste marker, and what it did

Franki put one song on him that day: **"17" by Youth Lagoon.** His own account
of its effect is the clearest statement of
[[wiki/mind/concepts/the-cool-metric]] in the corpus applied to a person in real
time — *"FUCKING NOBODY IN UNIONTOWN WAS LISTENING TO YOUTH LAGOON"* — and the
consequence he reports is total: it moved her from a list of ordinary attributes
he names flatly (*"brunette, skinny af, drug-accepting, daddy issues"*) to
somebody he *"immediately bowed down before to worship."*

Two things are worth separating here. The attribute list is a **type**, stated by
Dan as routine and recurring. The song is a **credential**, and it is what
converted the type into an obsession inside a single afternoon. Under the cool
metric, familiarity with an obscure record is not a preference but evidence about
how a person came to know things — sought, not received — and this is the
sharpest documented case of that judgement being made about a person and acted on
immediately.

## Gaps

- **The date is not fixed.** "Early July 2013" plus a fireworks display is as
  close as the record gets. A dated ATM withdrawal on Suz's account, or a
  Uniontown fireworks schedule for 2013, would close it.
- **The five-day figure is now attached to two different things.** See the
  contradiction recorded on [[wiki/people/franki-faris]]: the wiki has carried
  "a five-day rebound" as the length of the Franki relationship, while this
  capture attaches five days to *Alexis's stay at 155 Virginia Ave*. Both cannot
  be assumed.
- **Nothing here is contemporaneous.** The whole page rests on one 2026 capture.
  The July 9–31 2013 self-typology emails and the August 14 2013 post-mortem
  already in the corpus are the nearest dated material and they corroborate the
  window, not the day.
- **The hitchhikers, the ropes course job, and 15 Holly Lane** are single-source
  details with no independent confirmation anywhere in `raw/`.
