# 2015-2016 Annie Relationship Start

**Date range:** Nov 2015 - 2016

**Defining characteristics:** Breakup with Lex, meeting Annie, start of 10-year relationship. High emotional events: first dates, panic attacks, moved. Genesis of trauma-bond attachment architecture.

> characteristics and does not belong there.** The LIFE_EVENTS_CALENDAR row
> `2015-11-28: Lex cheated on me 2 weeks in after I moved her to fla` was
> generated by tagging a message on the date it was *sent*. The message is Dan
> narrating the **2009** beginning of the Alexis relationship to Annie, directly
> after *"I love this it's the exact opposite way I started my last
> relationship."* Per the operator the episode was online-only and unconnected

> classifies by message date and keyword, so any retrospective mention of a past
> event lands in the wrong year.

inferred from timing and content, not read directly off the row. With
that caveat, it stands as the earliest dated primary-source text of the
pivot itself, sent the same night, not a later retrospective account.
