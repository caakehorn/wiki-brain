# Feb–April 2025: Return and Rupture

The immediate aftermath of the January 2025 affair discovery and the
February 22, 2025 exit from the NYC apartment — an exit Dan arranged
with the landlord and presented to Annie as an eviction. These are the
specific weeks the broader [[wiki/timeline/periods/2025-collapse]] page
names only in aggregate. The page's primary source is a ChatGPT
conversation Dan ran on April 27, 2025, asking the model to act as "an
exclusively impartial arbiter of the fact patterns" over a ten-day
message log (April 17–26) between him and
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] — a self-authored, contemporaneous
analysis distinct from the later raw-CSV recounts that
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/dan-annie-fallout-verdict]] performs on the Aug
2025–Mar 2026 terminal phase. That source is both this page's greatest
strength and its largest hazard, and the 2026-08-13 pass is mostly about
the hazard: the arbiter was given a fact pattern with one fact removed.

## The real hinge: January 9–11, 2025

This period's declared window opens on February 1, but its cause sits
three weeks earlier, and the page previously did not name it at all.

On the night of **2025-01-09**, at 23:18 ET, a message arrived on Annie's
phone written by someone else: "Hey Dan / It's Eli / Annie is down stairs
/ I work with her" [RAW-DUMP]. That is the
[[wiki/timeline/events/eli-incident|Eli discovery]] as the corpus
timestamps it.

**The next day Dan issued an ultimatum: if Annie wanted the relationship
to continue she would have to quit her job and find another one** — the
job being the one she worked nightly alongside Eli. Per the operator, it
"was met with absolute indignant rejection from her, and it was at this
point which maked the true beginning of the terminal era of the
relationship. She was caught and still nto willing to compromise"
[OPERATOR]. Nothing else in the corpus dates the start of the terminal
era to a specific decision; every other account of 2025 starts from the
affair itself. The distinction matters, because it relocates the
breaking point from the betrayal to the refusal to restructure around it,
and because everything that follows — the call to Paci, the staged
eviction, the withheld rent, the return to Pennsylvania — is a response
to that refusal rather than to the affair.

The ultimatum was delivered by voice and left no text. What it left
instead is a hole, and the hole is measurable.

**January 10 is the only one with zero outbound messages**. Across the

singular by that year's [DERIVED, same scope].

notices (a Round-Ups summary, and a $10.88 Apple payment declined because
the card was locked). The other four are all from one number,
`+17243228715` — [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suzanne Frank]], resolved
through the unmerged Facebook address book, which carries six separate
entries pairing that number with her name and the `7243228715@vzwpix.com`
alias. They read, in order: "Is everything ok?" (02:14), "Hello?"
(12:22), "Are you alive?" (23:26) [RAW-DUMP]. None was answered. The day
the relationship's terminal era began is the day Dan's mother spent
twenty-one hours asking whether he was still alive, into silence.

### January 11: the ultimatum in writing, and the landlord pre-brief

Traffic resumes on January 11 and immediately carries both halves of the
plan. At 15:35 ET Dan wrote to Annie: **"I am calling John back now. I'm
not asking for extra time. I need to know TODAY what you want - you want
to be with me or you want to stay in New York"** [RAW-DUMP]. The word
*back* is load-bearing — it presupposes an earlier call, which places the
first approach to Paci on or before January 11, within two days of the
refusal.

Twelve minutes later Annie answered: "I'll be contacting John as well bc
I was not aware of this having to be out by feb 1." Three minutes after
that Dan replied "I was on the phone with him as you sent that," and then
**"And told him you would also be calling him to confirm"** [RAW-DUMP].
This is the verification call the operator's account turns on, visible in
the record from the other side and a fortnight before it happens: Annie
announces she will check with the landlord, and is told the landlord has
already been prepared for her.

By January 16, [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]] was pricing rentals in
Fayette County — "You can get a tolerable place for $800+ here. You can
get a nice place for $1,000. Keep in mind, you are going to need a
vehicle too" [RAW-DUMP] — seven days after the Eli discovery and sixteen
days before the eviction performance began on February 1. The return to
Pennsylvania was settled before the pressure that supposedly compelled it
was applied.

## The exit and the asymmetric landing

The financial facts this page has always carried are real and none of
them change. Dan describes roughly $10,000 already owed on the apartment
plus a $7,000 ConEd bill, and treats continuing to argue about staying in
New York as "not really even worth the time it would take to debate."
His account of his own posture is likewise borne out elsewhere in the
corpus: he never treated the affair as a "terminal blow," proposed
returning to Pennsylvania to regroup on family money, and deliberately
left the destination of the next apartment "intentionally empty" —
offering Annie any location on earth and undertaking to find the funds.

What has to be re-read is the frame around those facts. The shot clock
was not found; it was set. The offer of "anywhere on earth" was made
inside a situation whose only fixed constraint — the February deadline —
was Dan's own invention, which changes it from an unconditional gift into
a choice between destinations after the one destination Annie wanted had
been removed by arrangement. On 2025-03-02 Dan quoted her own words back
to her: "Do you remember the other day when you said 'I just said I
wanted to be in New York, not that that is going to happen'" [RAW-DUMP].
That is what a manufactured option set sounds like from inside it.

That landing was itself precarious: Suzanne Frank had declared personal
bankruptcy in 2024, and the childhood home — [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]]'s

my life that i could crash land in" — was listed for sale the same week
this conversation was written (a Thursday in late April 2025). This is a
full year earlier than the sale process [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank]]'s
existing housing-arc section documents (the 337 Saratoga Drive sale to

approval, closing around June 2026) — the same house evidently went

By March 1, 2025, Annie was fully established at her parents' in

operator's "she was staying in new york and that is final" describes her
negotiating position in February, not the outcome; what she actually
refused was the plan's fifth step, not the departure.

## The week in miniature: April 17–25, 2025

Dan supplied a day-by-day account of a single representative week,
asking the model to timeline it. The pattern that emerges is a tight,
repeating cycle: drug-procurement logistics (a supplier called "Bop," a
contact named John, [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]] often facilitating
delivery) generate friction over timing and money; Annie's caregiving
obligations — for a grandmother-figure referred to as "Sugie," plus
church and extended family — repeatedly compress the time available for
the relationship itself; Dan reads each compression as evidence Annie no
longer prioritizes him and periodically escalates to statements like "I
just want to know once and for all who I spent 10 years with"; short
repair cycles follow (an evening together, an apology, "I love you"),
resetting the loop rather than resolving it. Annie's own language in the
log names the caregiving load directly: "dealing with an old women's
piss," feeling like "a slave" and, elsewhere, "a servant."

Two specific frictions stand out as more than routine: on April 21, a
playful use of an AI Pixar-style photo filter turned into a boundary
violation when Dan continued uploading and sharing Annie's images —
including one with a third woman — after she expressed discomfort, and
he registers her objection but does not stop. On April 22, Dan explicitly
names his own depression and pushes Annie away ("leave me alone"); she
complies, then returns unprompted later that day and stays until 10 PM,
which he logs as a genuine repair. On April 24, Dan states the core
problem in the plainest terms found on record for this window: not
chores, not logistics, but that the relationship itself feels
irreparably "smashed" with no visible commitment to a future from Annie's
side.

## Reading it against the rest of the corpus

This week-level granularity is not available anywhere else in the wiki
for the Feb–April 2025 window; the existing 2025-collapse period page
covers the year at a much coarser grain, and the fully quantified
terminal-phase record on dan-annie-fallout-verdict.md begins in August
2025. What this page adds is the *shape* of the intervening months: the
same pursuit/withdrawal pattern the later terminal-phase statistics
measure numerically (74/17/11 abuse triad, the reassurance-dominance
triad, the volume asymmetry) is already fully present here in April,
four months earlier and with Dan generating the analysis himself rather
than the wiki reconstructing it after the fact — evidence the pattern was
stable across the whole terminal window, not something that developed
gradually over 2025.

What the 2026-08-13 decode adds is a second, colder reading of the same
document, and it is the more useful one for anyone using this source
again. The April 27 conversation is Dan asking a model to adjudicate a
fact pattern he curated, ten weeks after removing the largest fact from
it. The model's outputs — "unilateral," "a pivotal assertion of
autonomy," "avoidant behavior rather than partnership" — are conclusions
about a situation it was told had been imposed on both parties by money.
That does not make the pursuit/withdrawal finding wrong; the terminal-phase
recount reaches it independently from message counts, which is why it
survives. It does mean **every characterisation of Annie's agency in
this file is downstream of an omission**, and none of them should be
carried onto a page without a corroborating primary row.

There is a further, sharper reason to read it this way. In the same
conversation Dan writes that "one day in january i get a text to my phone
and from that moment forward - everything in my life has been just
getting progressively worse." The text is Eli's. He is dating his own
collapse, accurately, to January 9 — while describing the February exit
as something that happened to him. He knew the difference and did not
write it down, which is the cleanest available demonstration of what this
source is: a truthful record of how Dan experienced the period, and an
unreliable record of what caused it.

**Prediction.** If the decode holds, no source authored by Dan for an
outside reader between January 2025 and the 2026-08-13 capture should
name the eviction as arranged. The ones checked so far do not: this
ChatGPT conversation calls the apartment "given a shot clock" (April
2025), and the message record contains no outbound use of the word
"eviction" to Annie in the whole January 9 – March 10 window [DERIVED,
per [[wiki/people/john-paci]]]. The prediction is falsified by any
pre-2026 Dan-authored text — a chat log, a dossier prompt, a note —
that describes the eviction as cooperative or fake.

**Gaps:** the underlying message log Dan uploaded to ChatGPT is not
itself on disk in `raw/` — only this analysis conversation is, so the
April 17–26 week cannot be independently recounted. The final NYC address
is [[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st]] and the exit date is February 22, 2025,
both now established, but the exact monthly rent — and therefore the size
of the moving capital the two withheld months actually raised — is not in
the record anywhere. Suz's 2024 bankruptcy filing is still not
independently corroborated by a legal-record source, only by Dan's own
statement here. **The ultimatum of January 10 rests entirely on the
operator capture**; the corpus corroborates the day's silence and its
consequences but contains no text stating the demand, which is consistent
with it having been spoken and is not evidence that it was. Nothing in
Annie's own voice, at any point in the recovered record, indicates she
ever learned the eviction was arranged, and no message after February 2025
revisits it — so whether the concealment held permanently or merely
outlasted the corpus is unknown.
