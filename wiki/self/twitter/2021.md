# Twitter / X — 2021

Fetterman primary as a recurring object. GOP 'radical Democrat' talk, a restraining-chair bit, fundraising numbers. Political fandom with a handicapping instinct, not slogans.

## Narrative

**Thirty-four originals, twenty-two of them in January, and the January is the
best single demonstration in the archive of the thesis
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/vertical-authority-skepticism]] argues.**

**He works January 6th like a case, in public, over eleven days.** On the 7th he
starts from primary footage rather than from a side: *"the footage showing how they
got through each layer of security is just inexplicable"*, then a disclaimer of
standing — *"i have no background in security or law enforcement, to be sure"* —
then the observation an amateur can actually make: *"it doesn't take a criminal
justice degree or any training whatsoever to know that the 'mosh pit' is not a
reccomended move."* Later that day he states the hypothesis at full strength:
*"i'm as sure as i could possibly be that the capitol police had arranged to allow
the chuds into the building while they LARPed the violence they would usually be
committing"*. **And in the same tweet he audits it**: *"but what was the endgame?
is that being too generous"*. Then, more cautiously, on the same evening: *"the
Capitol police seemingly collaborated to let them inside"* — *seemingly*, hedged,
after the strong version.

**Then he refuses the symmetrical conspiracy, and the refusal costs him nothing but
is the whole point.** On 17 January, to someone claiming antifa staged it:
*"Sooooooo...are you saying that the radical leftists were agitating to keep the
far-right president in power rather than the democratic candidate? Do you seriously
think that antifa is working against their own ideological values to sling mud on
Trump supporters? Come on, dude."* The test he applies to the other side's theory
is internal consistency — the same test he did not apply to his own until he
audited it himself two tweets in.

That sequence — entertain, state at strength, self-audit, hedge, then demolish the
mirror-image claim on principle — is the mechanism the synthesis page describes,
and it is two years earlier and considerably more legible than the Nashville
debunk of 2023. It also runs against his own side twice: on 6 January he blames
*"The Democratic party and their corporate ring-kissing"* for the conditions, and
in June 2022 he is still working the same seam (*"there's something weird about
Jan 6 participants in red MAGA beanies specifically in and around the broken
window"*).

**The other January thread is the ideology from October 2020, holding.** *"just
wait until they are running against a principled and unapologetic dem soc"* (5
January), and the bit about a *"new primetime series that is just conservatives in
a restraining chair... and make them define terms they constantly use incorrectly
such as: socialism, globalism, obamagate, liberty/freedom"* — which is a joke about
definitional discipline, the thing he actually cares about.

**And the free-speech position flips, on a date.** 26 October: ***"Lenny Bruce was
arrested...not by 'the public', but by the state. Dave Chappelle is facing
criticism which is, I'm told, different."*** In January 2019 he called the
criticism of Louis CK *"fucking disturbing"*. Here the distinction between state
censorship and public criticism is not just accepted but wielded. The wiki has
dated this move to 28 March 2022; it is five months earlier than that, and the 2022
post is the elaboration rather than the turn.

> **A 273-day hole runs from 25 January to 26 October 2021** and it is a gap in
> the export, not in his posting. Nothing about 2021 volume, and no claim of the
> form *"he went quiet after January"*, may be drawn from this page.
