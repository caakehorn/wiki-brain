# Knowledge Preservation Philosophy

The wiki is a long-term semantic memory graph, not merely a collection of important facts.

The purpose of capture is preservation. Future analysis may discover relationships that were impossible to anticipate at the moment of recording.

## Preserve Before Compressing

Prefer retaining:

- mundane details
- proper nouns
- locations
- chronology
- uncertainty
- subjective interpretations
- emotional context
- seemingly irrelevant objects

A small detail may later become a critical connection point.

## Raw vs Canonical

`raw/` contains source records. Raw captures are evidence and should remain unchanged.

Canonical entries transform those records into structured knowledge objects:

- cross-linked
- expanded over time
- optimized for retrieval
- connected to related entities

## Entity Creation

Create entities when they provide future retrieval value, not simply because a word appears in a document.

Useful entity candidates include:

- people with recurring relationships
- meaningful places
- institutions
- objects with narrative significance

Avoid turning every incidental noun into a node.

The goal is a meaningful memory graph, not a keyword index.
