## Note

The queue's first entry, taken from the "persona slang" list on
`wiki/mind/concepts/the-cool-metric.md` to prove the loop end to end. It did
not stay a test.

## Reading

**`choom` / `choomed` appears once in 106,629 sent messages.**

```
2021-02-04 20:59  S  9165013615
So I was really choomed this morning and moved a bunch of stock that I
probably shouldn't have just because I was too high
```

That is the whole record. One use, February 2021, inside the market era, in
its literal sense — stoned — with the gloss supplied in the same sentence
("because I was too high"), which is what somebody does with a word they do
not expect the reader to know.

**The finding is not about this word.** Checking one word meant checking the
list it came from, and the list does not survive the check in the role the
page gives it. `the-cool-metric.md` offers *failson, garbage person,
shitshow, choomed, normie, Blue MAGA, choom* as "his own documented persona
slang" — the **observed** counterweight to the vocabulary that page rejects
as "safely quirky." Counted against the sender-tagged corpus:

Nineteen occurrences in 106,629 sent messages — roughly one every 5,600
messages. Three of the six are used exactly once, ever. For scale, the same
corpus carries `fucking` 1,745 times.

**`garbage person` is the one that is genuinely misfiled, and it is the most
used of the six.** Five of its six occurrences are self-directed and share a
fixed frame:

- 2018-04-02 — *"because i'm just a garbage person"*
- 2018-10-27 — *"Because I'm a garbage person"*
- 2018-12-17 — *"Oh act surprised, like I'm not a garbage person"*
- 2019-08-17 — *"i'm honest enough to let you know i'm a garbage person so you don't have to worry abo…"*
- 2023-10-30 — *"I am self aware enough to know I'm a garbage person who acts like an impulsive tod…"*

Only one is aimed outward (2018-10-27, *"I've already judged you as a garbage
person for not having an iPhone"*). This is not persona slang. It is a
**self-description with a stable syntactic frame**, deployed pre-emptively —
in four of the five, the admission arrives before anyone has made the
accusation. It belongs with the identity-by-negation counts the profile
already tracks (`i don't` 1,845, `i'm not` 814), not in a list of impolite
words.

**What this does not show.** It does not show the list is fabricated — every
term is really in the corpus, at the counts above, and `normie` at 7 is
ordinary low-frequency vocabulary rather than an absence. It shows the list
cannot do the job the page assigns it. Its function there is to be the
*observed* half of an observed-versus-selected contrast, and a list whose
median member appears once is evidence of the same kind as the curated
lexicon — words he likes — rather than of a different kind.

The counts are also floors, not totals: `bin/mine-messages` reads the
iMessage dump only. The Twitter archive is a separate corpus and would
plausibly carry `normie` and `Blue MAGA` at higher rates, since both are
platform-native. That would change those two rows and would not touch
`choom`, `failson` or the `garbage person` reading, which is where the
finding actually lives.
