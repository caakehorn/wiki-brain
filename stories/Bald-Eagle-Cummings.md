# Bald Eagle Cummings

## Summary

A defining childhood humiliation story involving Dan Frank's seventh-grade experience at Laurel Highlands Middle School, a sexual encounter before puberty, and the resulting school-wide nickname "Bald Eagle" / "Bald Eagle Cummings."

## Context

Dan was in seventh grade and had started school a year early, making him younger than most classmates. He also describes himself as a late physical bloomer and remembers being completely pre-pubescent at this time.

He had unexpectedly begun dating Britney Bolen, whom he describes as one of the prettiest and most socially prominent girls in his class. Dan remembers this as an early example of a recurring pattern he noticed throughout life: dating women he perceived as significantly above his own social or physical level.

## Seven Springs Background

Dan had an unusually deep connection with Seven Springs Mountain Resort. His first season pass was obtained before he turned one year old. His family rented winter condos before eventually purchasing unit K2 in the Sunridge development of the Villages at Seven Springs.

The family spent most weekends from December through April skiing. Many of Dan's closest childhood friends, including Jerad Friedline, Josh Brannan, and Jon Allen, came from Seven Springs rather than Laurel Highlands.

## Ski Club Event

Laurel Highlands Middle School operated a Ski Club that traveled by bus to Seven Springs roughly seven or eight times per winter. Students usually returned when the lifts closed around 9 PM.

For this trip, Dan's mother Suzanne drove Dan, Victor Fiano, Britney Bolen, and Britney's older cousin Alan. Because Dan's family owned a condo, they used it as a base rather than following normal school supervision restrictions.

## The Incident

During the evening, Britney indicated that she wanted to perform oral sex on Dan. Dan states that he barely understood the sexual meaning of this and had no understanding of what physical response was expected.

Around 8 PM, while skiing the front face of the mountain near Avalanche trail, the group entered an undeveloped wooded area between Boomerang trail and a residential access trail.

Britney attempted oral sex. Dan describes himself as completely pre-pubescent at the time, with no pubic hair and no physical response. The attempt ended unsuccessfully.

## Aftermath

By the next school day, the story had spread throughout Laurel Highlands Middle School. Dan became the subject of widespread ridicule and received the nickname:

- Bald Eagle
- Bald Eagle Cummings

The nickname continued among older students for approximately a year.
